CMT4 - Andromeda 9



111-132

Reports
Wreeeeee, they got both RLs, quad and pent at the start :( However we used our experience of the map to CS it a bit and tool up for next quad, and I used it to clear ledge. We held the map then and I thought it would be easy. But credit to HB they rushed every single quad brilliantly and made it very hard for us. In the last few minutes they retook the YA area and would probably have caught us given another 5 mins. GG. - by funky pirate superstars

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



107-105

Reports
SHITFUCKCUNTFUCKSHIT. They got the better start again, with quad, RA and pent, we got first RL. They killed our RL so were in the driving seat. But as on CMT4, we rushed the second quad and their RL guy ended up boring, and we killed their quad. I used the RL pack to start securing RA and things looked good. We held the map for the first 10 mins or so but we didn't really make good use of our quads and never had a lead of more than 15 frags, and this cost us. In the last 5 minutes or so, HB all had RLs and we had zip, and they closed on us fast. In the last 2 minutes we were all running around trying to dodge them (in fact I think I was on 8 health for about 30 seconds with an RL, just running around water/pent). Eventually Mario Bros got 2 of us at YA and we couldn't get the frags back. GG. - by funky pirate superstars

E2M2_TDM - Ogre Citadel - TDM Edition



169-162

Reports
Another horribly close game. Like DM3, neither side was ever ahead by more than 10 frags or so. With 2 mins left, HB were 12 in front. Limey got penultimate quad and got a few frags to peg it back to 6. Him and me both had RL so it was good, but then they killed Limey with sg. It was down to me, and when I took the last Q I had 10 frags to make up with 20 seconds or something. I got a few but the rest of them were hiding too well and it slipped away from us. GG. - by funky pirate superstars
