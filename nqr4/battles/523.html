Battle Info  2003-03-14 Division 5 - Week 1 (1)
 

 
Dreadful Offensive Squirrels 0 - 2 Swedish Super Cops
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



81-205

Reports
SSC: Took a long time before we started since they insisted to play on a bad polish server on both maps.. finally we agreed and played. - by NQR Admin

The Swedish Super Cops went out this thursday to catch some Polish bad guys.. the target was the members of the clan DoS. It all started on dm2. No side was getting a fullstart or any advantage but as the time moved on we managed to use our cop teamwork to get in position and start fragging the polish bad guys.. However in the end we did get a bit lazy and lost control but the victory was still ours. GG DoS.. - by Swedish Super Cops


Demos
-


  
 

E3M3 - The Tomb of Terror



51-248

Reports
SSC: They only had 3 players and we had to wait for their 4:th one for a while.. after a long time he arrived and we played without any problems. - by NQR Admin

The next map was of course our favourite.. e3m3 - The Tomb of Terror. The swedes are mostly known for their kindness but here on this map we really show our real side. They managed to get RA/RL position in the beginning but only after about 2 minutes we had it under control. And when we have control we never loose it.. we started to try to take over the middle and the low rl. We did suceed sometimes but the clan DoS never gave up and took it back sometimes. In the end we were able to lock the whole map for some minutes and spawnfragged away.. then suddenly the battle was over.. The Swedish Super Cops has once again defeating criminals just as in the old good days.. Thanx DoS for a nice attitude and good luck in the coming matches! - by Swedish Super Cops
