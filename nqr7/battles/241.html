Battle Info  2004-11-14 1A - Week 6 (6)
 

 
Hyphen 0 - 2 Clan MalFunction
 
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



93-220

Reports
We were just chilling at mircworld when suddenly hang0r enters #cmf and demands us to join get2net server, so we did. The game begins and hagge/dead collects the first rl and does some serious damage with it, as usual. Fortunately for us he gets a cpu-lag and we manage to kill him, and gain control over gl/ya. That's basically it, we kept the pressure on rl/quad/mh so they never managed to break through. - by Clan MalFunction


Demos

CMT3 - DeutschMaschine



120-162

Reports
So, get2net didn't have cmt3 so we changed to splatterworld. This was no big deal since we felt really confident on cmt3 having won ToT and HGC comfortably on it. Hyphen surprised us by playing a solid game and, to be honest, they could have won this one. It was more or less even for the first 15 mins when it happened: angua killed hagge at the pent with like 10health left and hangtime got hagges pack(?) and was just about to take the pent when anguas rocket connected and killed him millimeters away from the pent. I think it's safe to say that hyphen would have had a great chance to win this map with that pent. - by Clan MalFunction
