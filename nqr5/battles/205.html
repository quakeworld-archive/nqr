Four Kings 1 - 2 EarthQuake
 
 

E2M2 - Ogre Citadel



126-135

Reports
We were surprised to do this well on this map as we haven't played it for probably a year, or even more. In the end we shoulda won it but we didnt play too smart in the last few minutes and allowed them to get the frags they needed. Was fun to play a new map for a change :) - by Four Kings

We got a really bad start and they almost locked the first two min, but we struggled and struggled and finally got control and they started camping LG with 3 min left and a pretty small lead (20 frags or something). We fucked 3 rls on our first attack but our second was succesfull and we made some spawning and won :D - by EarthQuake
