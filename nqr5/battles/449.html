Battle Info  2003-10-16 Division 4 - Week 6 (3)
 

 
Edge 2 - 0 OLW
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



129-72

Reports
We had a good start here and build up a 30-40 frag lead right at the beginning. Most of the pents and rings were ours and we had control over ra-ring-sng almost all the way through this match. However we`d never really managed to secure the ya area so they could use that as a base and put up hard resistance by stealing many many quads. Fortunately we managed to maintain the lead till the end. gg OLW - by Edge

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



149-148

Reports
At this one we had really hard time believe me. After a messy start they got water-tele-quad secured and we had to hide our asses down at lower rl. Despite our minor control over the game flow we created massive defense at lower and managed to waste most of their rl guys who tried to penetrate and make a takeover there. I`m still wondering how we could win this it was kinda miracle i think. gg OLW and better luck next time ;) - by Edge
