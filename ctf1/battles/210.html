CTF2M1 - Mckinley Station



665-320

Reports
We don't really like ctf2m1 and rarely play it in NA. That said there was a strange feeling of confidence coming into this game having bested the Swedish team twice on this map an hour earlier in a friendly game. I believe this was the first time we ever played this without powerups so strategy was a bit up in the air to start. We got off to a pretty good start and had built up a solid lead while keeping the res rune under wraps. Then blood dog had one of his usual crashes. We basically only had 2 players for a small period of time cause one guy waited for blood to time out to grab his res rune. VVV took momentum and were able to chain some captures together to make the game interesting again. At some point unex dropped and was replaced by caes. We were able to take advantage of the small window of 3on4 play and pretty much sealed the deal by regaining most of the captures lost while blood was out. GGs VVV - by American Idiots
