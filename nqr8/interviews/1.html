Interviews  

  riker  2005-03-29  
Despite being seen as a somewhat controversial figure at times in QW history, riker is generally quiet and not so well-known in the community. Following his h!o!t! move from LA to GTG prior to NQR8, I caught up with him and asked a few questions...
 
1. Tell us a little about yourself.
Im stellan from sweden!
 
2. What do you enjoy doing in your free time besides QW?
xping in wow
 
3. For those not in the know, give a brief history of your QW career, clans etc
i played with la for the last few seasons, before them it was in Lithium.
 
4. It's difficult to remember a time when you haven't been playing with Lege Artis (or Disorder), so it's somewhat surprising to see that you've upped sticks and joined Griffins Tappra Gossar. Why did you choose to leave LA and what made you choose GTG as your new clan?
mainly because i got tired sitting on the bench, but also because GTG needed ppl to be active
 
5. You've been playing QW for a long time now; what's your motivation to play nowadays and how long do you see yourself continuing to play?
i just play for fun
 
6. What's your most memorable 4on4 match?
must be cmf - la nqr3 i think, was e1m2 decider..was a awesome game
 
7. Who has been the toughest opponent for you (both individual and clan)?
dag in 1on1, in 4on4 its probably mem or nabbe
 
8. Do you think you will win NQR this season?
hehe not sure, cmf looks strong with hib now, and la is good if they are active
 
9. What do you think of the current NQR system (divisions,playoffs,maps)? What do you think would improve it?
tb3 rules, but it seems to be inactive in div3b etc. maybe should have been less divisions..
 
10. You've done well to avoid being banned from NQR recently. Do you attribute this to ParadokS not being an admin or have you started to behave more?
paradoks did all in his power to hurt LA/disorder to win nqr. same reason he used hacked clients etc.
 
11. Who's the best 4on4 player of all-time, in your opinion?
memil
 
12. You're the coach of the Swedish all-time 4on4 team. What 4 players do you pick (not including yourself).
hm its diffrent for each map, for e1m2 it would be griffin, mrlame, nabbe, angua. dm2 is striker, mem, griffin, mrlame. dm3 is dag, striker, memil, nabbe
 
13. Now some non QW stuff... If you could sleep with any person, living or dead, who would you choose?
qhlan sassa
 
14. What kind of music do you like to listen to? What's your favourite film?
tool : hmm i like future dark thrillers kindof
 
15. Can you see yourself ever playing any other FPS games besides QW?
maybe, but dunno which one :P
 
16. Got milk?
4 real
 
17. Anything else to say? Any drama you want to start? :))
im the best player ever
