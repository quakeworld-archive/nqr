Battle Info  2008-03-17 Bronze Cup - Week 2 (6)
 

 
Quakeklan 2 - 1 SmackThatAss
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



217-85
Reports
Nothing much to say really, our choice of map, we controlled it pretty well most of the time. I managed to get telefragged with armor and rl/lg like 3 times to many.. Still, it was a GG. - by Quakeklan


  
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



157-179
Reports
We didn't really get a fullstart here, but we managed to kill first rl after a while, and then controlled the map pretty well for like 10 minutes. Then we totally managed to forget Low and we were slaughtered. They got almost complete maplock for a while, and they always managed to have like 1 rl more than we did. Last 1-2 min we managed to kill them, we where down around 40 frags, and we started to hunt them like chickens on fire. They started to slowspawn and hide. Still woudln't have been enough to catch up. They were better. Well done STA. //fog - by Quakeklan


  
 

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



275-142
Reports
A bit surprised that the scores looked the way they did. It didn't feel like we had control of the map at all, we did some nice quadruns, hunted down their rl-guys and won most sg-fights. Wp and gl in next game mates. //fog - by Quakeklan
