Battle Info  2003-03-31 Division 5 - Week 6 (3)
 

 
Bleed 1 - 2 Swedish Super Cops
 
 

E3M3 - The Tomb of Terror



-9-394

Reports
1:00pm 31st March. Dear journal i write this in the hope that, the events of last night can never be forgotten, and the truth will out. It was very cold on this fateful day, colder than normal, and one could suspect that something was awry. My clan mates and I (bleed), had just played a very good game versus vets, and so were confident of taking on anyone. We msgd alice for a game, and then suddenly, a player sporting the scc logo entered our channel. I was a bit perterbed upon the quickness he had entered our channel, it was not nomal. In the back of my mind something was not quite right, but i could not put my finger on it. After a quick chat we agreed upon a server, and after some brief talking with my clan mates about tactics, arrived there. However, upon entering the quake universe, we appeared in some mythical realm which the english dare not tread. On these shore this realm is said to be cursed, it is a realm of terror. I felt uneasy, as im sure my clan mates did. Just then something happened, which i have still not been able to explain. The television flashed on, it was on bbc1, and a news report was being played. Apparently there had been a prison revolt in sweden, the prison in question being a maximum security prison for the evil quake players, that followed shub niggarath during the time he tried to conquer earth. the news repor then went on to explain, that the police had been sent to try and stop the prisoners. In the last hour however, there had been no word from the Swedish super cops. There was rumour that they had now somehow become tainted by evil, so more police had been sent in to find out what happened to the scc. Hopefully they would arrive in time to save the SSC. I prayed they would.. The Tv flashed off, and i thought to myself that i ahd been dreaming or something. Had the SSC become tainted?. A message flashed on my screen, it was my clan mates, they told me what i was doing, and to ready up. I placed the headphones over my head, my hand slightly quivering, and once again immersed myself in the realm of quake. As soon as the match started, my worst fears hit me. The SSC were no more, what we faced i cannot put into words, was the evil malevolance of it is to horrid to describe. SSC within minutes had the upper hand, and we seemed to be just frag bait for their evil souls. I shouted to my clan mates: Brothers the SSC are host to some foul demons, we must purge them!, make them holy! This seemed to fall on deaf ears though, as one by one the evil cops slaughtered us in n unabbaited blood bath. echojin made efforts to cleanse their souls, but new the map too little, so was slaughtered at every attempt. Ferret tried to use his world renowned "ferretness" to rid the SSC of their foul demons, but ended up dying lots. Even jamo with his "Mern-jon-luck" could do nothing, and the evil SSC trampled all over Bleed, who Bled to death. GG player of the match Chojin - (only guy on +ve frags hehe) //krass - by Bleed

Right after our struggling with the edgers we got another blood trail. Sometimes the cops can't go home when planned since we had to trace the blood. The blood belonged some blood brothers who calls themselves bleed. We managed to locate them and took them in to our station. There we did question them real hard but they refused to answer our questions about the murders they had done on the old veterans (Vet's). We had to torture them in our tomb of terror and we did press them real hard and they did suffer alot, we even used our "lavabath" method as you can see on the screenshot. We almost reached our goal, having over 100 frags each. So it was totally a bloodbath but this time bleed didn't enjoy it. - by Swedish Super Cops


Demos
Krass(-B-)

 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



138-117

Reports
the next map choice fell to us, and after a little discussion, we felt that the best palce to purge the evil demos now resident in the cops would be the holy teamplay map DM3. The first Pent fell to SSC, not surprising because evil seeks out evil. Krass however was on a mission, determined that these foul beasts be exocised fromt the bodies of the SCC, and so went on a quad/ring rampage clearing the ra and most of the rl area. He managed to obtain the second quad, and things looked good, when with quad/rl was struck down by jamo, who had begun to stray to the darkside, by the evilness of the ssc. The next minute or so was a bit of a scramble over the red armour, but jamo, and chojin held it off wel, enabling ferret to armour up. then the 3rd minute makr hit, QUAD!, in all the confusion to get and hold ra they had forgotten the quad. Krass was nearest, so valiantly begun to stride to the quad, from the bridge, as every seocnd passed, the seconds ticked down, the quad would appear any minute, would he make it!?. As hea turned the corner he was pounced upon by 2 SSc's, but throught his true grit and determination he said "NEY foul beasts, thou shall not have quad power", and so hurtled himself towards the quad, quickly getting it before he was easily dispatched by the SSC. things then remained fairly even up to the fifth minute makr, when once again the 666 called out to the lost souls of SSc, and they were drawn to its ever summoning call. Ferret however was on a mission. He obtained quad ring, and went on a rampage clearing ra. Bleed now seemed to be more in control, and continued a steady frag increase, with jamo defending the red armour very well at times. Bleed were once again dumbfounded by SSC's skill in gettin the nex pent. they they went onto clear the RA, and now took the lead. chojin however held the ya, and allowed his fellow combatants to tool up, and then attack the quad. it was then chojin who got the quad and cleared the RA. It was then "ferret time", with ferret leading the onslaught against the SSC, who were beginning to wane, and took back the ra with some good quad runs. SSC managed to get the last pent, but with bled using some good teamplay and ferret running the quad well, not much could be done by SSC. GG player of the match ferrer -(nice quad runs) //krass - by Bleed

After our humiliation the blood brothers of bleed went to the abandoned base and started to prepare for a strike back on the cops. We knew that they could be strong on dm3 since they did beat Vet's on it. And the cops were tired since they had been up for a long time and had just recently returned from vacation. Bleed's preparation were good and the game was very even we managed to take some pents but we never had control of the armor area which was the reason why we lost. They got their revenge and they did deserve it since we played crap, btw nice work from ferret who did much of the work for bleed. - by Swedish Super Cops


Demos
Krass(-B-)

 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



128-156

Reports
aving just won dm3, we seemed comfortable going into the next match dm2. It was also quite apparent that the evil aura surrounding the SSC players was beginning to dim. If we could win this map, we could set them free.. the first 2 quads went to SSC, but they did not make use of them, the next minute or so was a frenzy over control of the map, but by about the 4th minute, bleed had control over tele and quad. It was then about the 10 minute that the "anti ferretness" kicked in. He went on a bit of a rampage making a few tks, especially against jamo :////. From then on, it was a bit of a scramble with scc slowly coming back into the game. The last few minutes were very frantic indeed,but SSC managed to come away the victors after securing the tele, and getting the alst few quads. It is unfortunate that we were not able to free these good cops of the evl that resides inside them. Hopeflly the next opponents, can purge the foul beasts from their souls. //player of the match Krass (nice.) - by Bleed

Now the cops were really angry after loosing a very important point that we might need for fighting for a top 3 spot. This created a bit of chaos among the cops and when the final battle between the blood gang and the cops were about to start the cops were not in a good mood. However we tried to focus on our task to bring this gang down and lock them in behind bars. We managed to suceed but it was close that they won this map aswell, we played shit and they played good. GG bleed and I am looking forward to more surprises from you against other clans. //dREk - by Swedish Super Cops
