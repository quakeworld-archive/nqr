Battle Info  2004-03-08 Division 2 - Week 8 (5)
 

 
MNY 2 - 0 Griffins Tappra Gossar
 
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



188-174

Reports
Game started sorta even. We got quad, they got gl, quad deaded, etc etc. About halfway through the game we had control and were fraggin nicely. A quadrun gone bad and 2 mins later they are able to get quad and secure the map. It was all downhill from there. I think our 30 frags lead with only a few mins to go would've been enough to def with, but the quake gods wanted differently. Every spawn went mny's way. They landed on every rl, and if they didn't, they lost the rl duel then respawned and landed on rl. GG. /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar
