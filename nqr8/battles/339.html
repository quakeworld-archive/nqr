DM3 - The Abandoned Base



143-179

Reports
We got the quad/ring and the first and second rl. Then we managed to gain a little bit control, then throwing it back to PE and then back to us. We had control of the map just before the notorius 10min pent. I specifically told Slaughter to come to the pent but he didn't listen at all and stayed for the ring. Meanwhile in pent Skisso killed an lg/rl nmy and left a pack for me. Then cage jumps out of the windows, kills me, Skisso and Stalk with rl and leaves with 7 health. He gets quad with that pent and kills Slaughter to ra. So we lost three rl's to that pent. That was definetely the turning point of the game. GG Slaughter for not helping with that pent. - by Satanic Slaughter Clan
