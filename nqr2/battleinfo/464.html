  2002-03-25  
Zetor vs 4Kings
     
  DM3  
   
  234 - 29  
 
It was time for the quarter-finals in NQR, and the battle stood between the finnish Clan Zetor and the UK clan 4kings. Lots of bets were going on before the game, of how the game would develop etc ;). ZR map of choice: Dm3. The game started with ZR getting pent and first rl. I think 4k got quad not sure. How ever the ZR Pent player went on to secure RA area wich he did without any trouble. ZR secured Quad area and were able to take 2:nd Quad aswell, he went on a manhunt..and mowed down several 4K players during his quad run. ZR got control over enire map from time to time, Rock has Ya under control and is taking easy frags while Hib is out on his quadruns. At the 17 minute mark the score is 33-7 to ZR. After awhile 4K manages to launch an attack vs Quad area, and Slash gets powered up with Quad..he attacks Ya from Quad and bags Rock at ya, ZR retaliates almost instantly and reclaims the YA area, 4k Quadrunner down. It's time for 2:nd pent and 4K assembles their troops at Pent area while awaiting the Pentagram of Protection. ZR attacks at the exact right moment, a great battle is faught around Pent area. But eventhough 4K players outnumber the ZR players ZR goes away victorius with Pent(ZR did one of thoose inpopulare but nice snatches 1mm infront of the 4k players). At the 13th minute mark the score is 68-16 to ZR, and now ZR really gets an iron grab on 4K, ZR controls all vital positions now such as; Ya/Bridge,Q and Ra..4K tries to fight back but when they fight vs the greatly tooled ZR players they dont stand much of a chance. Time for 3rd Pent, and ZR gets that without to much trouble, and the carnage continues led by Hib. At the 8 minute mark the score is 120-15 to ZR they got a total lockdown..lots of spawnfragging. At the 6th minute mark 4K manages to get Quad and they launch an attack vs Ra area wich they claim as theirs after a short battle with the Ra Guard. ZR retaliate through ring enterance and kills the 4k Quadified player. 4K fight brave but they can not really do much vs ZR on DM3, 2 minute mark 193-23 to ZR, Game ends 234-29 to ZR.

  DM2  
   
  172 - 158  
 
Now it was time for 4K's choice of map: DM2. ZR get's a really nice start, they get low RL and Quad. He gets a few 4K'ers with his Quad. 2nd Quad and 4K attacks through Bigroom/2xYa and they manage to get the Quad, he frags some ZR players but catches a rocket with his face at High Rl, 4K quadrunner down. The following minutes ZR secures Ya,Water and Quad and does some fragging Quadruns down in Low Rl Area, 4K retreat to 200/Mega. At the 16 minute mark the score is 48-14 to ZR, however 4K builds up at 200/Mega and launches an attack vs Quad area, the 4K players tooled up to their teeth with RL and Armor, a furious battle over Quad area rages, 4K manages to win the battle and grabs Quad. However the 4K quadrunner's is greated by some rockets marked with his name from telly/ya area, fired from the ZR Ya Guard. 4K don't let down on the trust because of that though, they assemble their troops once again, and manages to get the next Quad aswell. He dies rather quickly too though, so ZR still control the most vital positions of the map. At the 11 minute mark the score is 104-40 to ZR. 4K retreats once again to 200/Mega area, setting up a trap for the ZR Quadrunner, he blast a few around the corner but misses his mark, then he jumps round the corner..and gets blasted to bits by 4K. With ZR quad runner down they got the time they needed to tool their player up again, ZR knowing this was setting up defences for the anslaught about to happen at Quad/Water area. 4K'ers launch a great attack vs Quad area and the ZR player desperatly tries to defend their claimed area but are forced to retreat. Slash grabbes the quad, and mowes down all resistance left at water area. He then moves on to low area knowing that the time is against 4K. He sweaps the Low Rl area clean of ZR players, and the ya/water guards gets some easy frags. And im thinking, "OMG is 4K actually going to win this one". The 4K players fight as their was no tomorrow throwing all they got against their foe Zetor. Zetor is beeing pushed back all the time and any retaliations by ZR is quickly regained by 4K. For ever frag ZR gets 4K gets 4..or something like that, the game is really getting tense. ZR is fighting furiously to hold on to the last position they have under their control, 200/Mega. At the last Quad ZR knows they must get the last Quad..and a absolutly supreme and great battle over Quad area starts...37 seconds and counting score is 167-152 to ZR..After all the mayhem ZR gets the final Quad and can get the breathing room they needed. With that last Q they secure a ticket to the semi-finals of NQR playoffs. Great Game both clans, and really nice faught by 4K. Report By: Legio!
