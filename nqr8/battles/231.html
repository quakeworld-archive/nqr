Battle Info  2005-03-23 1 - Week 6 (3)
 

 
Insanity 0 - 2 Griffins Tappra Gossar
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



174-228

Reports
Bad start for us as Insanity catch a fullspawn. First RL secures RA and the second goes for YA. We rush the YA guy and manage to create a borepack with some shaftaction - promptly spawned by Riker. That was the start of the turnaround for us, as the quad produces another pack (despite being kaputted). Insanity played well, producing plenty of weaponry to challenge our control, and never truly letting go of RA. Most of the pents were stolen by SG guys and ended up doing practically nothing, and quads went roughly equal (11 vs 9). It was a good game, but in the end we ended up keeping a tighter leash on the spawns than insanity did, and it produced the fragdifference we needed to pick up the second DM3 of the season. GG! //dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar


Demos
All Pov (MVD)

 
 

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



108-287

Reports
Well, a bit weird start to E1M2, as whoever spawned start didn't go for RL and it ended up being picked up off a secondary spawn by me. First quad died early, and I picked up mega then went to YA. Some good GL action from an insanity player nearly got me, but a borepack is produced for mrlame who manages to secure YA. He runs out of rockets however, and can't make it to second quad in time - it's killed again. By this time, mrlame is joined by griffin who picked up second RL and we have as safe a control on YA/GL as you can hope for this early in the game. Third RL gets picked up by Insanity, but promptly bores into a GA guy and we get that one as well. Not until the 3rd or 4th minute is Insanity able to shuttle an RL out to Mega for a foundation to build on. Eventually the game pans out and Riker/Griffin/Mrlame are able to lock down most of the map, leaving me as broester and feeling like the fifth wheel. We kept Insanity on the low end, and the fragcount shows it. 13-7 in quads and 42-14 in YAs essentially tells the story. GGs guys! //dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar
