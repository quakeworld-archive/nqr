Reports

115-187

Reports
Okay time for the decider, not a surprise there is a decider map when the MAN OF THE BIG GAMES: Martin "Siv Ler" Fang is in the server. He just likes to push the nerves of the opponent and see how far they can go. After that he can give them the "Nice try punk" -diploma and a bonus pompous laughter! Siv got a fullstart at the beginning like with rl+pent+ya+quad+ring+ra+lg+gl. After that he did some nice trickjumps from penta water to sng. You guys really should check it out, its was like gordon freeman moving siv with gravity gun. Naturally fom got like a bit scared seeing that siv is now playing for real and demonstrating his godlike nervs. They put up some nice resistance, but in the end it wasnt enough. We had ra most of the time and managed to take enough quads and the last pent to secure the victory. Now the only question remains, do they get the diploma from mr. Martin? - by Clan MalFunction
