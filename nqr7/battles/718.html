Battle Info  2004-12-20 Div 1: 1/4 final
 

 
Griffins Tappra Gossar 2 - 1 Fragomatic
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



172-162

Reports
Well, for once we got a bit lucky and scored a fullspawn. Pent picked up second RL after some help from Quad and managed to deliver it to RA without much issue. Second quad went our way safely, but died soon after from a discharge. Pack given to mate however, and we kept control. All was swell until the 4th quad or so, at which time we had run out of weapons and lost quad to the 'matics. Luckily we had a good show for pent, managed to swipe it in front of the enemy RL who kindly provided the necessary firepower to fuel the powerup. We retook the map and this time we kept RLs in the family to keep the kids happy. All was fine once again, up until third pent when shit hit the fan and fom managed to steal it. They had 2 rls at pent as well, and manu bored one for xantom's pent, but it was swiftly stolen. Valla hesitated in boring his, and as such fom lost time - time they didn't have. Pent didn't have enough left to secure RA, and was chased all the way to water where he finally died. -2 RLs and no RA control for fom after third pent. Ah well, shit happens, eh? After that we sorta screwed up and gave away alot of quads, eventually leading to our loss of RA right before the last pent, which was swiped through the use of some bondeskill, which probably won the map for us even though pent didn't have a weapon. We managed to hold on 'til the end, though it came close. I think this was the first ever DM3 we won in NQR7. GG guys, and given 'matics skill on DM3 I don't say that lightly. /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar


Demos
All Pov (MVD)

 
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



199-204

Reports
Well, E1M2 as usual is pretty chaotic, but when you toss in niclas with a GL and mrlame with Quad you've got a stew that's bound to overcook. We started pretty well, keeping constant pressure on fom RLs and showing up in force for quad. Somewhere along the way we eventually lost our focus however. We spread out more and more over the map, didn't provide backup for our RLs, and just didn't show up for quad when it was due. Fragomatic exploited our newfound weakness without mercy and were able to squeeze out just enough juice before the last minute to prevent us from catching up. Well played guys, you're the first ones to have defeated us on E1M2 in an official game - and we plan to make sure it's the last. GG! /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar


Demos
All Pov (MVD)

 
 

CMT3 - DeutschMaschine



222-135

Reports
Someone called CMT3 boring. I call it different. We tossed CMT4 and 'matics were obviously uncertain about DM2 after having lost DM3 and played so even on E1M2 that they reasoned their chances on CMT3 to be better. The game started fairly much in our favor, and on CMT3 that's saying alot. Had the spawns been reversed it could very well be fom that were on their way to the semis right now, because they didn't play all that badly. The map is sorta like DM3 in the sense that you can gain alot from the first minute of the game. Had fom known their spawns a bit better it might've not been so easy. GL-spawn got RA, RL-spawn got quad, and god knows which spawn got pent, but the 'matics were just left with YA and Ring - a poor selection to base your decider map on. We kept control of RA virtually throughout the map and kept pressure high on fom to keep them from buildling up an assault. Now and then they managed to get through to RA, and grab the odd quad, but rare was the occasion when they got two in a row. Better luck next time fragomatic - you were, so far, the hardest win we've had in NQR7. /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar


Demos
