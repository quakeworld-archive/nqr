Battle Info  2004-11-21 3B - Week 6 (7)
 

 
Osams 1 - 2 Assassins
 
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



143-224

Reports
reports by gaz: ass' map. I got RL, mega etc and went to YA where I got flooded and killed (no pack) so things were reset. I think we got next couple of RLs but ASS are awesome at flooding and moving around in groups. They locked up the RL pretty tight for the first mins whilst Petter ran around with quad and the rest of us charged around like headless chickens. Eventually ASS got YA/GL safe and had a vice-like grip on it for the last 7-8 mins whilst Inferno ran quads. GG - by Osams


Demos
-


  
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



182-117

Reports
Our map... ASS got RL and pent, lovevib got ring->quad->RA and I got hillmega->shaft->2nd RA. lovevib killed the 1st RL, who kindly left a pack, and together we attacked YA on 0:35 to kill the 2nd RL, who also donated a pack to the Osams RL shelter campaign. From then it was pretty tight and we relied on some nice quad runs to clear out their RLs. A few times they got into RA but we did a good job of clearing. Despite forgetting the last pent, we were able to rack up the frags in the last few mins and make the score look a bit more comfy. Toughest DM3 so far! - by Osams


Demos
-


  
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



142-146

Reports
Eeeeek... they threw CMT3 and we threw CMT4. Tb3! Huzzah. I got low->secret->quad and they got high just as I was moving down the quad-way. I splatted him (no pack) and went to secure tele. However, I cleared high first and by the time I'd killed a few spawns at tele, I'd run out of rox and I accidentally bored whilst SG fighting by walked over tele rox and fired RL :P From there, ASS had a firm grip on tele/quad and were making good runs to low. With about 8 mins left, lovevib and lunna made a daring assault on quad and cleared the map. We had a good lock and pulled level on frags. Then ASS hit back again, slowly killed our RLs, and just before the 18 min quad, I got telefragged by team mate (I was last surviving RL). They got the last quad and they were 2 frags ahead. lovevib killed 2 of them (incl quad) and we were level on frags, but then lovevib got ambushed (our only RL at this point) and they were left to pick up a few cheap frags at the end. Tight game and fun (mini-whine: clox had horrible lag spikes through last part of the map and I lost my sound after 10 mins :(((() but GG! - by Osams
