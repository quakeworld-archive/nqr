Battle Info  2003-10-22 Division 6 - Week 2 (3)
 

 
osams 2 - 0 Vampire Reborn
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



152-50

Reports
We performed well at DM3, securing the RA and obtaining most of the quads. DM3 has been somewhat of our weak map since me and WhyZ started playing since we are new to Quake TDM and haven't played any of the "non 2on2" maps very much but this all worked out just fine and there was never any serious problems keeping VR back. They did make some attempts and managed to take the precious RA for a while but we managed to clear them out with the quad almost instantly. - by osams


Demos

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



140-96

Reports
DM2 was a little bit tighter and our team play was not as co-ordinated. In the very beginning the scores were equal but we managed to get a small lead which grew larger and larger. VR came back late in the game but there simply was not enough time left to do anything about it. The poles had 2 people with 100+ ping from the beginning but looking at the screenshots that seems to have been taken care of by the end of DM3. DangerDanger in our team also suffered with a 130 ping connection throughout the game. Good games anyway, thanks VR! - by osams
