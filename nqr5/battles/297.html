Battle Info  2003-11-23 Division 2 - Week 4 (6)
 

 
American Gangsters 2 - 0 America Must Die
 
 

E3M7 - The Haunted Halls



213-69

Reports
AMD gambled a bit in my opinion and picked E3M7 as their home map. The starting spawns finally for once on e3m7 went our way and we got the first PENT and QUAD and ran it coast to coast immidiately making tons of frags and securing both the PENT-RL and the FORT-RL . We didnt have much trouble sustaining full control and after the 10minute PENT it was clear no-matter how badly we could fuckup in the second half of the game we could always "cs" it out in the final minutes to get the win. AMD did manage a few sporadic attacks and retake the FORT-RL once every couple minutes but it was not enough to stop our QUADs attacking from PENT side. - by American Gangsters

CMT4 - Andromeda Nine (CMT Version)



193-59

Reports
We chose CMT4 as we knew that AMD lacked exeperience on this map, and we were quite confident we could pull it off prior to the match. Riding on good spirits we were able to secure the key points of the map early on and maintain a solid lead. AMD was unable to mount satisfactory attacks on the QUAD as we always tripled up on them. KovaaK put in a great performance at red armor where he was constantly under boomstick fodder and still managed to survive continously thwarting AMD's comeback chances. Yogic also put in a killer team effort with his extraoridinarilly high ping. 39 Sakuraba forever! - by American Gangsters
