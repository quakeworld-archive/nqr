Battle Info  2003-11-17 Division 2 - Week 6 (6)
 

 
American Gangsters 0 - 2 FUDOH
 
 

E3M7 - The Haunted Halls



59-275

Reports
The initial five minutes went quite well for us as we enjoyed a slight lead in the scores and solid control over the YA-RL. Meanwhile, FUDOH was holding on to PENT-RL and trying to clear us out with QUADs which did not seem to work out. The match inevitably came to a dead halt for us at the second PENT, which we were unable to snatch away from FUDOH's firm grip. The enemy PENT cleared out YA-RL and FUDOH held on to both RLs for the remainder of the game. To the best of our efforts we were unable to mount a satisfactory attack on YA-RL. FUDOH once again showed they are the e3m7 specialists. - by American Gangsters

CMT4 - Andromeda Nine (CMT Version)



96-157

Reports
The game didnt start off very well for us and we fell behind on scores quite early. FUDOH held on to YA while we were frantically stealing RL's from RA and trying to mount attacks on QUADs in between. The game looked fairly hopeless with about 10minutes left, when we finally retook the YA and got some control back. We were slowly but surely making up the frag deficit and came as close as a 20 frags to tieing up the game with a few minutes left remaining in the game. However we died somehow and got spawnfragged the final minutes of the game :) - by American Gangsters
