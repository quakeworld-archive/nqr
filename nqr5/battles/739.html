DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



89-189

Reports
First NQR game and first clanwar for our guys in 3 years (except for one prac :P) They spawned lower rl, but we trick jumped and took quad+upper rl and Danno had a blast with that fragging around, the rest of us fvcked up though so we still didn't manage to take the next important rls so sta took over and got some quads and took the lead. After a while we somehow took over and after Danno got quad again we managed to get the map under control with mostly spawnfragging for a while getting a lead. That lead we managed to hold on to when sTa started attacking and created some panic in the team. At the end they had upper and quad under control but not nearly enough time to take our lead back. // Pyta - by 42

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



95-134

Reports
sTa took the start here getting pent, quad, rl, yeah almost everything :) After a while of them getting most of the frags but not quite controlling the map we managed to turn around and hold on to RA while doing quad runs clearing out the rest of the map, and that way we played for a while getting back in the match and eventually winning it. As in the last match they took control of most of the map when it was a little more than a minute left but it was almost over by then so we can blame on that ;) What has to get better for next game is pent timing, they got three pents when we, if we had timed it better, would've had at least two of them. GG sTa it was a really fun game. // Pyta - by 42
