205-139

Reports
Warning for Paranoia...use real nick!! - by NQR Admin

After that very boring cmt3, we switched over to dm3, their choice. Para spawned ring, quad, rl and lg. We got pent. Unex with pent hunted down first rl at ya and caught him by surprize. VVV took next rl and lg and gathered at ya. Para flooded ya which resulted in VVV's rl going down. So 2nd quad existed of a fight between Para's lg (200/130) and VVV's lg (150/100). VVV took the quad during the pov contest and killed para's lg. After that the lead slightly increased with vvv holding onto the ra most of the match and para holding onto ya. Most q's and pent's went vvv's way and resulted in a VVV win on dm3. - by Veni Vidi Vici
