Battle Info  2004-03-02 Division 2 - Week 5 (4)
 

 
Griffins Tappra Gossar 2 - 1 Fraggers United
 
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



200-172

Reports
The game was played on Z since iop had been acting funny and bbb was too unstable for the lot of us. Due to our high pings (28-40 + griffins standard hpw ping) we requested that a minping 27 be set to even the odds a bit. FU accepted; very sporty of 'em, thanks guys. E1M2 started with team quad and rl for us (late rl, noone spawned it), but fu got the YA but didn't manage to keep it for long. We got the 2nd RL as well but I'm not sure what happened to those when it was time for 2nd quad. FU got semigood control of the map by the time of third quad. It broke however when we managed to sneak a RL through spikes to take over YA, and later Quad. Map volleyed back and forth like a good ball in a tennisgame, eventually to settle in our favor long enough for us to secure a good enough lead to win it. GG! /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



135-170

Reports
Well, DM2 started totally in our favor. SR spawn deluxe. We got both RLs and Quad, but when our quadder went deep into low he made a mistake (oops!) and br?stade a bit too much. They used that RL and the low rl well to break our control and get a quad, with which they secured the high part of the map very well. For a while it looked grim, but we managed to pull together all at the same time and get ironlock control of the map. So fast it even surprised some of us; ermac got a quad and griffin told him to secure and not attack - but voila, we had 2 RLs beside the quadder and high rl was waiting to be picked up by our 4th guy. Did I say ironlock control? Well, the iron was rusted, cus FU did a good attack only minutes later and broke our control. After that it was downhill. They kept quadding low to steal RLs and interrupting our attacks through bigroom very well - they really deserved this win. Well played guys! /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar

E2M2_TDM - Ogre Citadel - TDM Edition



188-157

Reports
battle of the titans? //oosi - by NQR Admin

I assume some people will go "wtf? e2m2tdm for decider?" when they see this. Well, we've been sucking badly on dm3 lately, and we've also been wanting more cmt4 ever since that tight game against SSC, so we went ahead and removed DM3 from the decider draw. Alas, FU decided to remove CMT4 and we got kenyamap as result. After some whining from grf about how he was no good on this lpb-kenya-map he goes ahead and wins it for us. GG bondeskills! It started in favor of FU, although I'm not entirely sure who got what, but after a while into the game they had RA locked tight and wouldn't let us get close to quad. We kept killing their quad whenever it peeked out from GL/Quad area however, so the game kept pretty tight. I think at some point it was tied 125-125. By that time we had overtaken the RA area though, by repetitive shaft attacks from water and eventually gotten the right timing on quad. GG on a bad map guys, better luck next time. /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar
