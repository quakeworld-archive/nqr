E1M5 - Gloom Keep



193-183

Reports
We all feared axe on this map, knowing they have played it long and beaten many clans on it. It was a tight game from the beginning to the end, axe having most quads but never managed to get map lock. GG - by EarthQuake

CMT4 - Andromeda Nine (CMT Version)



124-195

Reports
This game was exciting aswell! After two minutes axe was leading and we were pretty confused since we not often get beaten on this map. The game turned after 10 min when we got hold of ya area and began to close in on axe. We got last pent, continued to hold ya-aera and got most of the last quads which resulted in victory. gg - by EarthQuake
