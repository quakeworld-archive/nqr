DM3 - The Abandoned Base



118-144

Reports
Sux to write reports the day after because I forget so much :). Anyway, dm3 was their map choice. The start was kinda even I think, we took rl and they got eyes/quad but was killed by our rl. Don't know about penta, maybe them :). Anyway they took control of RA in the beginning but we turned it around to get up a quite nice lead, but somewhere in the middle of the game they turned it back again and kinda spawnfragged us for a while :/. The last few mins were quite even with us getting a little more frags but not near enough to make a comeback. Felt we could've won this map with a little more tp, a little more skill and a little more luck ;) - by OLw
