  Quakeworld.nu Done Extreme!  030926  
 

Quakeworld.nu started as a pet project and has been running for about 6 months now with great success. We have been working very hard the last 3 months recoding the site and we now have a solid base to build a really nice Quakeworld community site. You can read about the facelifts and the new features here

QuakeWorld.nu is a site that strifes to be the center for the true quaker. We bring you QW news from all over the world, articles and have a nice forum where our users can speak their mind.
One cannot deny that we are mere voulenteers. We are not professionals and we do not get paid. We have alot of ideas to make the site a better place for those who wish to play and commute with fellow quakers.
We are aware that trying out QW and becoming a part of the QuakeWorld community is not easy at first, and in relation to that we want to make it easier eventually. But we are trying to find a balance of suiting the needs of the current community aswel as taking initiatives for newcomers. It all take resources, but we are confident that with constant development, dedication and commitment we will bring QuakeWorld up to a higher level.

Go visit our site @ www.Quakeworld.nu
