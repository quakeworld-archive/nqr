  LA.Striker  2002-11-19  
Lege Artis... do they really need an introduction? I got some words with one of their members (C)Striker, and here's what he had to say.
First tell us something about yourself name, age, country etc.
My name is Juan Aranda and I'm 17 years old... and live in V?xj?/Sweden.
 
You have played almost all of your group games now... and you have not yet lost a single map which is really impressive, whats the secret? :)
well I don't really know...dont got any special secret :) ?
 
I have seen you lost prac games though, what is the difference between practice game and official game to you? Do you play more serious in official games?
well, we play more serious in offical games of course.. in pracs it doesnt matter if you win or lose cause you always are trying on new stuff or
something like that :)
 
Alots of people like to call Lege Artis - Flaming Fist, I think its improper since dag is the only FF member still active in LA, what do you think about this?
what I think of this its that we are not like ff.. because its only dag and akke are real ff..
 
If you reach finals in NQR3 who would be your dream opponent? and why?
dream and dream... but it would be fun to play against cmf or hf... hf becuase they're good players and such a nice guys :) and cmf.. cause they are fi's and i really hate them cause they're so good :P
 
If you dont reach finals in NQR3 which clans do you think will?
I well think between sr/cmf/hf/tvs and fs.. but most on sr/cmf
 
What clan do you fear most in NQR3? and why?
it gotta be hf ..cause you never know what they're doing! and they're really scary when they got a good day :)
 
What do you think about NQR?
well, I just play!! .. but okej, its a fun leauge...good with div1,2 and 3.. :) so you play against guys who are at your level :) and that you meet every team before playoffs..
 
Is there any things that you would want to see in the future of NQR?
its good like it is.. like I said I just play :) !
 
Thanks for our time and good luck in the rest of NQR3/SD5 and of course QHLAN5 any last words?
np, well.. DIE-silver are the greatest! :D and kisses and hugs for hf and la !! h?h? :)
