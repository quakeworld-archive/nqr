  tVS-Hib  2002-09-22  
Hib became one of the champions of NQR with the Finnish clan Zetor last season. But after the clan dismantled for various reasons, Hib decided to head back to the place he once called home: the Viper Squad. He is a strong tp player with some great skill, and with tVS's otherwise impressive lineup, many are tipping tVS for great things this season. Here is what he had to say when he talked with legio!
Who is Hib?
My name is Lasse Parviainen and im 21 years old. I live in a place called Varkaus(semi Russia) in Finland.
 
How long have you played QW? Shortly describe how it all started :).
I started playing for about 5 years ago with regular quake. How it all started? Well i started a new school and there i saw a few dudes playing this game on Lan and it seemed like a cool game so i tried it out :)!
 
What are your system specifications?
Currently i have xp+1800 whit 512ddr radeon8500 whit ms explorer optical usb :E.
 
What kind of improvements would you like to see in the future NQR League?
Sponsors, and money prizes would be cool, if possible a LAN Final :). And after tight games, we should get beautiful girls taking care of us :). A few more maps to choose from would also be good i think.
 
Do you like the new design/functions on the new NQR site?
I havent got really in to them yet, but the overlook is good and im sure u guys will do great job just like last season!
 
How does it feel to be back in tVS?
Well it?s nice cozz i know blaze and ihminen irl and xamp and mirage are nice suckers too..;)
 
Did you expect a tougher fight from HellFire in the final? And how did it feel after you beat them :)?
Heh, yeah they were kinda soft but we had no preasure cozz we were pretty inactive back then, but ofcourse it was great to beat them in the NQR Final.
 
I guess tVS will be kind of the defenders of the "crown", after ZR-tVS Fusion. What do you think your chances are to go the whole way this season?
It's definetly going to be harder this time. Since im the only player from the champion team i guess u could say that
tVS is the crown defender. I think tVS chances to go the whole way is pretty good, eventhough im the only player from ZR we still have a strong team in tVS.
 
Which clan would be the dream opponent in a NQR Final, and explain why?
erhm tough one ofcourse any sucky clan would do heh ;), No but slackers or cmf would be nice. But then again it would
be sweet to face Hellfire once again :P!
 
Finally thanks for your time, and good luck in NQR3.
Np, Thank you!
