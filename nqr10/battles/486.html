Battle Info  2007-03-06 Bronze Cup group B - Week 3 (5)
 

 
Clan Tayto 0 - 2 EnemyQuad
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



79-116

Reports
This was the strangest game we played so far. I dare say we played flawlessly for 10 minutes with the RA area locked down. We didn't give them any frags for the first half of the game and had about 100 frags. We were happy campers over at RA/SNG when we suddenly realised, oh hell it's pent-time! Our expertly crafted plan of keeping RA safe just collapsed in a moment of collective amnesia. Tayto's pent killed us all basically. There is only one thing you can do in this situation. We bravely slowplayed the next 10 minutes to victory. - by EnemyQuad


Demos
All POV (MVD)


  
 

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



99-198

Reports
This started as the usual FFA but when we got going with a few RL we were doing better and better as the game went on. Most of the time we had 3 RLs and me (antic) running around like a headless chicken with the ol super nailgun. We kept to YA/GL and all three RLs were tied at 51 frags right before the end. Well played team. And well played Tayto and good luck in the tournament. - by EnemyQuad
