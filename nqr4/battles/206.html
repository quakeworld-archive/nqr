Battle Info  2003-03-09 Division 1 - Week 1 (1)
 

 
Slackers 2 - 0 The Axemen
 
 

E1M5 - The Gloom Keep



198-171

Reports
We had nice tactic talk before the game and followed tactics like 4-5mins and then something happend and we totally forgot our tactics and last 15mins was ffa at rl area. ParadokS got like 50% of quads and won the round for us. - by Slackers

The Axemen got a great start with SNG, YA and RL, but they fucked it up and 1st RL got killed, so first Quad did not get as lethal as it could have been. Slackers really knew the tactic on this map, so The Axemen got a real hard fight on their home map. Slackers got a 20-30 frags lead early on, but no team was in control. SNG/YA area shifted teams often, and no team managed to get some good lockdown. Both teams were good at flooding a nmy RL without armour, so it stayed kind of "chaotic" most of the time. Slackers played well and managed to get a good lead far into the game, getting many Quads, but with about 3-4 minutes remaining the norwegians suddenly got SNG-RL-QUAD control and started a nice little comeback. But eventually Slackers stroke back, distrubing a possible good Axemen lockdown, and that did it for the NQR4 favourites. gg both teams! - by The Axemen

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



275-120

Reports
Slackers got the best start with a RL-Quad after a chaotic start at Quad area. But no lockdown, so The Axemen were still into the game. But as time passed on Slackers proved to be better than The Axemen on controlling the map, so the frag-gap got slowly but surely bigger. After about 12-13 mins The Axemen managed to turn the tables around, traling with about 70-80 frags, and suddenly had a strong lockdown. The frag-gap got smaller and smaller, but in the end The Axemen lost telly and it was a 50/50 game again. So in the last few minutes of the game Slackers increased their lead once again and made many more frags than the Axemen. gg both teams! - by The Axemen
