Battle Info  2003-11-20 Division 2 - Week 4 (6)
 

 
FAPPERS 2 - 0 Griffin's Tappra Gossar
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



147-108

Reports
GTG got fullstart but still didnt really got the map in control. 2nd penta was totally forgotten by FAPPERS but somehow they managed to get the 3rd one. Fappers were leading by ~10 frags before the last penta, which "surprisingly" went to GTG. With the penta Griffin took quad and cleared ra so it should have been clear victory to gtg? BUT since we have such players as omny playing for first game this season it eventually became GG. - by FAPPERS

Well, we've always been kinda sucky on dm3, but I think this game introduced a new meaning to the word suck. It was that bad. No, it was worse. Fappers didn't need to be told twice, and took their opportunity to score an easy dm3. /dak - by Griffin's Tappra Gossar

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



187-148

Reports
Fappers not much interested of the quads, eh? But we had made tactics for this map and changed keeo to buggy who was bit icy this time, still getting his usual >50 frags and ofcourse Vore is soo hot at the moment.. and it seems like jester is finding his "joy of the game", maybe its because omny is playing again. - by FAPPERS

Decided to go with e1m2 once again, but something was missing today. I dunno really, just wasn't our day. Faps kept coming with RLs to YA when quad was just gonna clear out mega, and so forth. Timing and teamplay totally sucked as many of our quads got wasted on spawnfragging while we were loosing the map. Not writing down fappers here however, they played well and deserved the win, I'm just a bit annoyed we didn't put up a better fight. Ah well, next time! :) /dak - by Griffin's Tappra Gossar
