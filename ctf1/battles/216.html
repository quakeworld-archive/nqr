Battle Info  2005-02-20 2 - Week 2 (2)
 

 
MPOS 1 - 2 qw.ger
 
 

CTF8 - The Strongbox



596-505

Reports
Our tactics were clear, mad about parrots superstar@defence, gaz@gaybar playing the holding role in midfield and snkfknrussian shootin for the hoop. Our Russian@caravan.nl superstar dominated their defence and they had to play in big room a lot. The gaz@gaybar bigroom show was way2hot4them and we won this map way2ez. - by MPOS

Well, first of all, we would like to thank mpos for playing 3on3 instead of 4on4. Our 4th had to leave, unfortunately. Back to the game. Their mapchoice was CTF8 (quad on). They had the better defense in the first 10-15 minutes with strength rune, so we could hardly get some captures. Quad was enabled, which was, if I remember correctly, very balanced. No one really couldnt really do something interesting with it. We got some captures in the last 5 minutes, but we couldnt take the lead. - by qw.ger

CTF5 - Da Ancient War Grounds



136-603

Reports
At the start we argued about who would be the sniper, and eventually we decided that snkfknrussian should do it, so gaz@gaybar and mad about parrots looked after the bomb. After about 15 minutes of trying to plan the bomb in the red base we realised that we weren't playing CaptureTheCounterStrike and that we were seriously fucked. SD danced around us with their crazy flying men and invincible attackers, we had no chance. - by MPOS

CTF5, our map choice, quad enabled. We had str + res rune at start (didnt we?), got quad and the first capture was made. At about the 10th minute, demon couldnt spawn anymore (ffs?), so he had to reconnect, which took 2 minutes, since the server was full (greetings to the specs). They didnt really have a chance on this map. - by qw.ger


Demos

CTF2M3 - Spill the Blood



64-1060

Reports
Finally we were clear on what mod we were playing and so we were all set for victory. We played with quad off but this just mean that the start was all-important. We didn't have a single rune in our base and they got strength/resistance early on. We tried valiantly for 10 mins and then gave up to go and chat on IRC. - by MPOS

CTF2M3, decider. MPOS took the lead at the beginning, but we had str + res rune, followed by some caps. Due to a telefrag by cyrax (yup, fragged the one who took the flag, indeed), and some wild "ok, shooting the middle is good!"-action (with str rune) someone of mpos asked if the server records demos (haha :]). They went ALT-TAB, so demon and cyrax went setting a new "fast-cap"-record :D (0:13 is ok for div2 aint it? ;(). All in all, good games and another thanks to mpos! - by qw.ger
