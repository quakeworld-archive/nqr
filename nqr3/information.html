Information  

 

Welcome, you have found the home of Nations Quake Rank. This is a QuakeWorld league for all clans in North/South/Central Europe. Nations Quake Rank is built so that each clan can play their battles and then self add the result, this is done by a login which your clan will recive when signing up for NQR. When the game is added by your clan an NQR admin will
confirm the battle and the rankings will be updated.

Nations Quake Rank is running the 3-2-1 point division system. There will be 3 divisions, running for the "elite" clans (Div.1) to "less skilled" clans (Div. 3). You will fight every clan in your division, you are only allowed to fight the same clan once each season during regular season. After the regular season ending the top 4 teams from Division 2 will play the bottom 4 teams from Division 1 for a spot in Division 1 next season: 8 teams fighting for 4 spots. The bottom team in Div 1. will met the top team of Div. 2, second team from bottom will met the runner up in Div. 2, etc. The winner of this qualification match goes to Div. 1 next season. The match will be a 5 map game, first to win 3 maps prevails.

Playoffs: the top 8 teams in Div.1 go to the playoffs, fighting for the crown. . #1 vs #8, #2 vs #7 etc. The final will be a 5 map game. The same will apply between Divisions 2 and 3.

Points in regular season:

You win a game that ends: 2-0 = 3 Point(s)
You win a game that ends: 2-1 = 2 Point(s)
You loose a game that ends: 1-2 = 1 Point(s)
You loose a game that ends: 0-2 = 0 Point(s)

If teams are even on points in the table, the following will seperate the clans; The game between them decides, mapscore, amount of maps won and finally the fragscore.
