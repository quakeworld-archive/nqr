  ...and four games on Wedensday  030403  
 

In division 3 Quadrangers managed to get a couple of games played. They first played vs their countrymen Maniacs and QRNG won the first map, dm3, after a pretty tough game. Then [0] showed skill on e1m2 and grabbed a point vs the division favorites. But QRNG got back on track on decider map, dm2, and secured 2 points.
QRNG second game was vs FUDOH from Norway. The Norwegians camped down the Swedes on e3m7 and secured a point. However on dm3 and decider dm2, the Swedes were too strong and QRNG made 4 points this evening.

Division 4 saw a game when Unidentified Quake Entity from Holland faced off with the PsychoHaloons Crew from Poland. PHC won a very close one on dm3: 99-96, very low scores for a 4on4 game on that map. Then it was e3m6 and the Dutch tormented them selves to victory after a good game. Decider map, dm2, went easily to UQE and they collected their third victory in equal many games, the Dutch clan wants to mingle with the top clans in this division.

In division 6 St?l-Kalle Klanen from Sweden played hannovers Head hunter from Germany. This was a one sited story where the Germans ran over SKK two times on dm2, with vast margins. SKK will be looking for points when they meet the lower half of this division.
