Battle Info  2004-12-19 Div 3: 1/4 final
 

 
Osams 2 - 1 Apocalypse3000
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



123-115

Reports
reports by gaz: osams' map... they got RL and he got to RA without any problems, quad died and petter got pent but he didn't last more than a minute or so. the first few minutes were hectic and just at the key moment lovevib timed out so we played very briefly 3on4. Luckily, superstarmannydoks was on hand to jump in for a few minutes and got 12 valuable frags until lovevib came back. they held RA for most of the game I think, but we made some nice quad runs and made use of our control. Then in the last few minutes we hid (not the first time this NQR - hi RD, fnu) and despite the scoreboard saying that a2k were ahead slightly, we knew that manny's frags would be there to save the day. - by Osams

CMT4 - Andromeda 9



114-133

Reports
a2k's map; we got fullstart (quad, ra/rl, ya/rl, penta) but couldn't use it well and i think they got the next quad. a2k held YA most of the game and got most pents. the quads were split fairly evenly but we didn't do a good enough job of taking over at YA and that's what won the game for a2k. - by Osams

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



225-106

Reports
We were convinced that A2K would throw DM2 because I can't recall them ever beating us on it in pracs... maybe they fell for my little white lie on my coverage column about us hating it ;) but anyway, they tossed cmt3 and we gladly threw e1m2. start was even, lunna getting low and adde getting high, with petter hopping to quad. they had a little control of tele hear and there and managed a few quads, but petter and lunna soon got their hands on the big blue shiny thing and fucked their shit up, yo. gg. - by Osams
