Battle Info  2003-10-06 Division 6 - Week 1 (2)
 

 
osams 2 - 1 Morituri
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



131-126

Reports
DM2 was very exciting with various tactical mistakes by both teams. Morituri was ahead in the beginning but we managed to, somehow, get some quads. We controlled the map for a while but Mori came back in the last minutes but with the help of some praying and slow spawning we won with 5 frags. GG! - by osams
