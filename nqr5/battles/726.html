DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



125-156

Reports
Picking a server was quite hard. I think we passed about 5 when poff didn't want to look any further, we proposed more than seven times if they wanted another one during whole match but they wanted to play (they had 10-20ms more and two of their players had ping spikes to 80/100). Poff got low-rl and clnd of sTa took the other rl and quad. We smacked their asses in the first half of the game when we got bored and had a nice warmup for their map in the lava. Seven minutes later Huli-Man found our secret boilingbath and scored twenty frags in the last three minutes cleaning his tube from the evil sTa atmosphere. (prt 70%, huli 47%) - by SmackThatAss

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



134-171

Reports
On e1m2 poff took control of gl/ya from the start as we were too high to find ya, after six minutes saka was high too when he bored at mega-roof.
