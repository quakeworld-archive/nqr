Battle Info  2003-10-12 Division 2 - Week 1 (2)
 

 
Griffin's Tappra Gossar 1 - 2 America Must Die
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



144-141

Reports
A game that started all in favor for AMD, and they managed to use their control well. Eventually we fought back however, and it turned out to be, as Hagge stated below, an exciting game! If only the AMD quadder with 30 seconds to go had known that at 200/mega there was 3 SGs and one rocket-less RL, AMD would've won this one :) /dak - by Griffin's Tappra Gossar

E2M2 - Ogre Citadel



133-182

Reports
E2M2 is a map I think I could learn to like. It is sorta 'dull' in the sense of RA/quad area being in possession of "everything" that's important on a map, excluding a good weapon (which isn't a problem to get there with, due to the many routes to pick from), but when noone is able to really lock it down it grows intense and interesting. In the end however, AMDs routine on the map paid off - they pulled away somewhere in the middle and kept the lead up 'til the bell rang. /dak - by Griffin's Tappra Gossar

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



93-169

Reports
To sum up, pent kept us in the game but RA won it for AMD. Simple really - we were incapable of getting an RL to guard RA at the same time as we were in possession of the quad, and eventually, AMD broke the threadthin control we had over the map and flipped it over. They never really let go after that, and when I wasn't able to pick up a weapon with the third pent (and left AMD in possession of RA after 30 seconds of wabbit hunting) it was game over as they pulled away to a secure win. Well played AMD. As a closing statement, creds go out to Griffin who must've summoned some divine presence as he kept going for 3 straight games, playing with 150-400 ping (closer to the latter whenever there was an enemy around), without quitting on us. /dak - by Griffin's Tappra Gossar
