Battle Info  2006-05-01 Silver Cup Premier - semi-final
 

 
osams 3 - 0 epic
 
 

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



221-133

Reports
(gaz) With our starplayer and captain Petter Lakso (c) back from holiday in Florida, he was quick to order us about and tell us our lineup for each map. As I had to go out approx. 1 hour after game start, and I was in the E1M2 lineup, we started on what turned out to be Castle of Osams. Seriously, this is perhaps the best we've played in a competitive game no this map. We got entrenched in YA/GL early on and made it really hard for them to get into the game. Manny & I did the dirty work by camping YA and flooding Epic's RL guys while Petter and Benny shared the majority of the quad running. Epic didn't play as aggressive as I thought they would and that helped us stay fresh between quads. - by osams


Demos
-


  
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



267-112

Reports
(gaz) This was insane. Epic flooded like crazy and made it really hard for us to completely settle into it. After 12 mins or so I was sure that the scores were fairly even, cos it felt like our control of water/tele was being knocked away all the time. Somehow, we were leading by 100 frags and I think it was partly down to great low RL timing and stealing by Petter. Occasionally we fully locked it down but they were always causing us a few problems at high RL. We were able to win by so much due to great quad running and good pressure at low. *saknizzen tyrone@tele* - by osams


Demos
-


  
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



274-49

Reports
(gaz) I didn't see most of this, cos like I said I had to go out. Anyway, Manny got pent, Benny got LG and Harsi got RL. Benny killed Harsi and that left Manny to stylishly move to 2nd quad and help us get a foothold at RA. It was the usual scrappy stuff for a few minutes as both teams tussled over the RA, but when I left after 4 mins we were just starting to kick into the routine, and it looks like we did a great job. So, like CMF we've made the final without losing a map. Wunderbar will prove to be a bigger test than Epic I think, but these Epic chaps are really very friendly and I would love the chance to prac them some time soon <3 GGs och kram, spread the love vibration \o/ - by osams
