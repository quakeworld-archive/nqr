Battle Info  2004-02-24 Division 2 - Week 1 (4)
 

 
Dutch Hope 2 - 1 Chosen One
 
 

E2M2_TDM - Ogre Citadel - TDM Edition



272-119

Reports
We had the largest problems with finding a good server for the game. They wanted to play on IOP where they had ping 12/26/26/60 and we had 4x 39. Then we wanted to get to Boomtown but that is down, EA gave snake ping 39 and he began to whine that it's unplayable, but later came back on that. Anyway we ended up on a dutch server where we rerouted to ping 39 and the pings were pretty close here. The game was quite messy they did a nice job taking over ra/q. C did some nasty quadruns with rl/lg cleaning rl but we had control most of the time and were able to take the map home with quite large scores. - by Dutch Hope

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



125-133

Reports
We saw their previous games on dm2 and knew they are very strong here. The game took off and they got Q+high-RL and got low-rl too. Secured the map right away and had control for about 12 minutes I guess when they were leading with 100-15 when we were able to takeover and with 5 minutes left we had low-rl safe too. But it turned out to be too late, they were playing slowly not rushing anymore (of course they wouldn't be th?t stupid), we were able to come very close but those 8 frags were just too many. - by Dutch Hope

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



177-173

Reports
They got the Quad but we killed it right away, Aki got the first RL and I think we got the first YA too but I'm not sure about that. Anyway, we got early control and fragged our way to something like a 110-50 lead when they were able to takeover. We were just playing the game out when Purity started to camp/time RL with five minutes to go and the last two minutes we were CS'ing at start. They were attacking all the time but they only had one RL left (they weren't able to take any) so they couldn't attack in a normal way. We were saved by the bell and won with 4 frags. Good game without too much whine after the bad start with 100% flame. GL and HF in the rest of NQR - by Dutch Hope
