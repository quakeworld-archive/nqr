Battle Info  2008-02-17 Bronze Cup - Week 1 (1)
 

 
Quakeklan 0 - 2 Naim
 
 

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



113-322
Reports
Since Godis and Lurq has been inactive a couple of weeks we decided to first play a dm3 as a pracc to see how we were doing overall. Pracc didn't go very well so we decided to pick E1M2 as our map, thinking we might have a chance to beat'em here. Ooouh we were wrong. Didn't matter how many armors or RL's we took, Arnette was seen all over the map with Q and just wiped our team out over and over. WP NAIM and especially Arnette. GG //fog - by Quakeklan


  
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



116-178
Reports
DM3 was their choice. After the earlier pracc on dm3 I understand the decision. This time we played a lot better, being able to snatch 2-3 pents, and taking down several RL-enemys with LG from center. We had a lot of chances to turn the game, but somehow Arnette showed up with Q LG or Igggy put a rocket in your face, well backed up by the rest of the team. Felt like it could end either way first 15 mins, but they managed to get control and the defeat was a fact. GG's Naim! //fog - by Quakeklan
