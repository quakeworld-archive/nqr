  Some minor changes to the playoffs  040412  
 

After some discussion amongst the crew we have made 2 minor changes to the playoffs:

1) In the gold playoff, the winner of the qualifiers will be "reversed" in terms of their opponents. I.e. Q1 will not play the div 1 1st team, but the div 1 4th team instead. This means that it's Slackers vs Antiquad or Team Freedom rather than Slackers or Hyphen or Nobbs R Us. This should become clearer once the full brackets are up.

2) Due to coding difficulties (i.e. we don't have a coder), and our noobish skills, we will be removing the loser's bracket from the competition. This is because it complicates the coding side of it as much as any other reason.

If anyone has any major complaints about these issues then don't hesitate to come into irc for a chat.
