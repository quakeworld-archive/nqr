Battle Info  2004-02-25 Division 2 - Week 4 (4)
 

 
Nobbs R Us 2 - 1 Griffins Tappra Gossar
 
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



121-135

Reports
Our map choice, we took control over the important GL/YA area pretty fast, though some FFA boomstick ala e1m2 were makeing some chaos in NRU. GTG managed to get some superguns and camped us out at MH/Start a tactic we've never seen before. smart move by GTG. We have met them before but without UL in their team, so we kinda underestimated their skillz. GG - by Nobbs R Us

Game ended up very close and interesting, despite us sucking. UL and Ermac kept gl/ya/quad area as safe as possible, and fought for every blue powerpill, griffin was there breaking his neck to get em as well, and I was working that RL/mega/ga area. Whole game felt like walking through quicksand every step of the way. 4x13 pingers are annoying on e1m2. /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar


Demos
-


  
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



144-137

Reports
Their mapchoice! they managed to gain full controll over the map and started their spawnfragging... we lost our hope and then siv broke the ice in our team by telling us to calm down and attack together, by some miracle we managed to slowly gain points and retake positions and when there were 15 seconds left the score was 137-137 and overall we could say it was a verry GG! - by Nobbs R Us

If we sucked before on e1m2 it was nothing compared to what we did now. I know it's kinda lame in general to whine about luck and things like that, but it felt like at every turn that little margin was always on nobbs side. It's hard running uphill for 20 mins, and with less than a minute left we were actually in the lead. I hate loosing a game on things like that, but there you are. /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar


Demos
-


  
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



185-119

Reports
The final decider map, the match lead went back and forth between us but in the last 5 minutes we managed to grab some quads and sweep them of the floor... gg! - by Nobbs R Us

Really never had hope for the decider as we had e2m2kenya and cmt4 as options to dm3. None of these maps suit us, and we didn't feel like playing kenyamaps, so we agreed to dm3. In the end, the 13 ping shafts broke the control for us too many times to count. We kept trying, UL working his butt off to get a few quads and try to take over RA with, but they kept coming back a few mins later with a powered up shaft to grab the next quad. Well played nobbs, and GG's. /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar
