  More clan news and some div changes  060305  
 

Bronze cup group A clan Bukkake Samurai announced that they need to quit NQR due to lack of players. This brings some chages:

Vitun Kova Bosse from Silver group C lost quite a few of their key players and we consider them to be Bronze standard. Therefore they will move down to take BS's place in Bronze group A.

A new clan, goldiggers, ran by ex-vkb players Domin and Kippo, will replace VKB in Silver group C.

Fixtures are not affected by these changes.
