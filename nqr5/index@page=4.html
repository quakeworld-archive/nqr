  IMPORTANT! Members and Homepage!  030929  
 

Some clans have still yet to add members to their clan, we STRESS the importance of this now, and give you 1 last chance to add your members to your team.

What is also essential is an working homepage, doesn't have to be anything fancy, just a updated member list with contact info.

Clans who has not complied before 24.00 CET Tuesday, will be deleted! If your clan's homepage is under construction plz notify the admins, and atleast add your members to NQR page!

Taken from rules page "Your Team":

"Your team needs to have a working homepage with updated info about all active clanmembers. There are no limit to the number of players in one team. Teams not fulfilling theese requirements can be forbidden to enter the Nations Quake Rank."

Also players registered with a different nick then they use to play with, should have this marked on their member page on NQR, for example; Leg (Legio!) <- beeing the nick you play with.

Playoffs

Playoffs will be a bit different from last season, this season the 8 best clans from Division 1 will advance to playoffs. Due to time shortage, and other issue's there will be no "mini-playoff / relegation games" for the lower divisions. This is sad, but not much we can do about that now. We will have some kind of "carrot" for you players/clans in the lower divisions next season.

If you have any question's regarding anything you are more then welcome to contact an admin at #NQR.

// the NQR Crew.

  Quakeworld.nu Done Extreme!  030926  
 

Quakeworld.nu started as a pet project and has been running for about 6 months now with great success. We have been working very hard the last 3 months recoding the site and we now have a solid base to build a really nice Quakeworld community site. You can read about the facelifts and the new features here

QuakeWorld.nu is a site that strifes to be the center for the true quaker. We bring you QW news from all over the world, articles and have a nice forum where our users can speak their mind.
One cannot deny that we are mere voulenteers. We are not professionals and we do not get paid. We have alot of ideas to make the site a better place for those who wish to play and commute with fellow quakers.
We are aware that trying out QW and becoming a part of the QuakeWorld community is not easy at first, and in relation to that we want to make it easier eventually. But we are trying to find a balance of suiting the needs of the current community aswel as taking initiatives for newcomers. It all take resources, but we are confident that with constant development, dedication and commitment we will bring QuakeWorld up to a higher level.

Go visit our site @ www.Quakeworld.nu

  Signups are open  030915  
 

Yes indeed, its the moment you've all been waiting for! Signups for season 5 is now open, and will remain open until Sunday 28th September - so get your application in while you can. You may wish to read the rules as there have been a couple of changes, most notably a change to the points system.

It is important that once you have signed up and your clan has been approved that you login and add your players as soon as possible.

Click here to signup.

Note that the signup script doesn't give you any confirmation, so try not to signup twice. It will take the admins some time to check your application before you are visible amongst the signups.

To start with, everyone will be entered into Division 1, so you can check all signed up clans there.
