Battle Info  2004-10-31 1A - Week 9 (4)
 

 
Hard Guns Crew 0 - 2 Clan MalFunction
 
 

CMT3 - DeutschMaschine



36-317

Reports
plz :) fun round :) - by Hard Guns Crew

CMT3 has been our haven as of late so we felt comfortable choosing it. We knew HGC were pretty strong on this map but after doing so well against ToT we figured we had a chance against HGC on cmt3 as well. The start was as fair as it can be, we got everything while the more skilled HGC-team only fought with boomsticks. After a while our dynamic duo hlt/fix took the map under THEIR control and HGC could only get a frag here and there with some amazing grenade/boomsitck combos. All in all, a great match between two teams who are familiar with cmt3! - by Clan MalFunction

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



112-177

Reports
We thought we could take this map home, and everything looked good in the first minutes we were in the lead for a while, but then something went wrong, we lost a couple of rl's gave them quad and had to retreat to low.The score was even for 2 minutes or so when nothing much happened neither side attacked but then some cs quad runs gave them the lead. When we finally built up some rls we were always too late to attack quad, and I sucked too much to do anything, guess i was nervous cause my hands were shaking. BG :| // billy - by Hard Guns Crew

Everyone in the clan was rather disappointed in fix's performance on cmt3 so we decided to bench him. Replacing him was no other than the man himself, the man of big games, Martian! After some minutes HGC were in control and fragging like a moonshadow in heat. Heroic effort by the man, Martian, turned the game around. As expected, he cleared the whole map all by himself and left his teammates some packs so they could get some frags too, tnx 4 that martian! HGC has been the greatest clan of all time on dm2 so it was a bit surprising that we could beat them here, but when you add the sajvler-effect, its not that surprising anymore \o/ - by Clan MalFunction
