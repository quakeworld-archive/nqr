Battle Info  2004-12-19 Div 3: 1/4 final
 

 
Assassins 2 - 0 Hippushnik
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



181-136

Reports
So, the game was off and the hippies were sneering at us for getting it all except for pent. This turned to laughter as their quadded eyes pulled the lucky show on our pent and got the second rl as well. These two rl's were soon to be dead and we got a hold of ra. Next 15 minutes turned out to be a game of even fragging and with infe getting like 10 quads we compensated for lack of weapons and did our best to keep ra-area. Just before the fourth pent hips had built up a slight lead by about 20 frags and seemed to finally lock it up quite well. Not giving away too many frags before the upcoming pent, we had a teaparty with our only rl at yard. This one died with all of us as the finns made a good wipe out, but graciously fedde pulled of the geronimo jump with sg and snatched the last pent, which turned the match into our favour. The last three minutes we managed to lock the map up tightly and with callen doing a great job timing ya/rl and denying both sauna armor and weapon, we got about 50 frags during the last four mins. GG! - by Assassins

yes we got the typical hips fullstart with first two rls and stuff and then bored them to enemy. we got a bit lucky staying in the game but the matoman aka the jebalafan was feeling ownageous and fragged enough to keep it tight till the last pent. - by Hippushnik
