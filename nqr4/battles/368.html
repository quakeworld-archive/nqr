Battle Info  2003-03-09 Division 3 - Week 1 (1)
 

 
New Wave 0 - 2 EuthanasiA
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



81-217

Reports
first game in a long time.. the start was pretty even, they getting the ra/quad area and we getting the bridge/ya area. I remember we manage to get the 2 first quads, but in lack of skill and tp, we didnt do anything good with it. after that they had some control over RA with 2 rl guarding the ra/ring area, but 4 mins into the game dexter manage in some magical way to grab the quad from them, and took out some rl enemies around quad/ra/ring, while I sneaked from lift/sng/ra. And now we finnaly could start pumping up those RA armors. The game was pretty even still, but when the 2nd penta (5min) came, vortex used his uber skills to grab it, and started his mayhem around bridge, while I got the quad/ring and started my mayhem around bridge also, after sending vortex away to secure ra. Now we finally got the upper hand is this game, and nw started attacking RA really hard, from bump, from sng, and every possible way. I think they used every trick in the book, to get control over RA again. But what NW didnt know was that I hixen, wrote the first edition of it (goljat & striker wrote the 2nd, and 3rd) anyway.. I used my quad, to clear every attack they made for about 10 minutes, while my teamates had ya,ring,ra secured from time to time. While the manage to get the last penta, and some control back, the frag diffrence were to big, and the last 1-2 mins of the game, we had gotten back that control. The scores dont tell the truth about this game, there were many times of this game, were it was very even. But then again, when we had control, we used it very well. Great Game nw, and good luck in the future - by EuthanasiA

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



127-213

Reports
next round. I thought I would skip this round, so tjilpi our new player could play, but dexter was sick, and didnt feel very weel, so he left instead of me. This was Tjilpis first qw dm game ever (atleast what I know of) before the game we told Tjilpi that his task was to camp lower RL, and hide and shoot enemies at ra/ssg. But what we didnt know was that he can't speedjump, so before the game tjilpi trained the ssg to ra jump for over 30 mins (he still can't speedjump).. well back to the game, it started out with NW getting a fullstart, atleast what we thought, after when I looked at the demo. I noticed that tjilpi got the first rl at lower and went to camp his spot :) anyway, nw used there good start very well, and soon they had the very quad/water/tele area under control, and they were increasing there lead fast, after 2-3 mins they were leading with over 30 frags. Then we finally started getting some rl's and manage to fight back, and around 6-7 mins into the game, panic started camping teleport, letting me grabbing quad. First I started securing, high rl, and around water/ra and teleport. But everytime I had secured lower, i manage to bore with quad, in some strange way :) but panic played very good at teleport so every time I died I had free armor/rl at tele/high, so I could start attacking again for the next quad. this game was pretty similiar to the dm3 game, the only diffrence I think was that when we got quad, we manage to get alot of frags, and while they got it, they didnt get so many. another great game, and gl - by EuthanasiA
