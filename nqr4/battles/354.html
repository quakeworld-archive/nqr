Battle Info  2003-04-11 Division 3 - Week 4 (5)
 

 
griffin's tappra gossar 2 - 1 Quadrangers
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



136-151

Reports
Quadrangers showed us right off that this was gonna be a tight game. I screwed up and somehow forgot how to jump (spawned ya) and gave their lift the pent, that gave em the 2nd rl which lead to an unsteady lockdown from RA. The map then wavered a bit, back and forth, and I think with only the last penta to go we were nearly tied. We managed to get that last penta, but they had obviously played hide and seek before and kept their rls tightly away from us. Good game QRNG! //dak - by griffin's tappra gossar

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



145-128

Reports
I have no idea what we did at the start todays dm2. I'll tell you one thing though - I was scared we were gonna loose 2-0 today for a while there. But then, just like it did against EQ, all the bricks fell down right for us, and we swiped the rug from right under QRNG's feet! We went straight from 50 vs 100 frags to 100-100 in the next 5 minutes, and kept on going. In the second last minute or so they did a good job swiping quad from under our noses, and secured the map. We fell back and bunkered up, waiting out the clock. Good game! //dak - by griffin's tappra gossar

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



147-111

Reports
After a shaky start we finally got the map down and after a few minutes of spawnfraggin, quadrangers bunkered up in RL - not much use though, we were in lead 100 vs 60, and we just waited out the clock, making occasional dashes at their flanks. They did a good job in the final minutes and took over the map from us, but why not earlier? If QRNGs had moved instead of bunkered, by the time they got the map they might've lost 20 more frags - but had twice as long to make it up. The map got kinda slow with their bunker in the middle, but I can understand their fear of griffins uremoves! :D GG QRNGs. //dak - by griffin's tappra gossar
