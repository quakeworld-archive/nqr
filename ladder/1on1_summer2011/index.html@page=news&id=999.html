Clarification of team set-up for 2on2 ladder
2011-04-15 15:06 by gaz
You can add as many players to your line-up as you like (i.e. more than two). This way, you can play many more games within your clan, if you want to. Or, you can sign-up separately. For example, Slackers could sign-up as one clan with members Paradoks, Murdoc, Zero, Striker and Krab. Any two of those five could play in a match.

Or, they could sign-up with two teams, with, for example, Paradoks+Murdoc and Zero+Striker+Krab.

The only restrictions are that you cannot switch players mid-match (even between maps) - the same two players must play on all maps, and players cannot be in more than one clan at a time.
