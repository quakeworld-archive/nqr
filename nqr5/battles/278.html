Battle Info  2003-10-11 Division 2 - Week 1 (2)
 

 
FAPPERS 1 - 2 American Gangsters
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



92-91

Reports
FAP spawned with first QUAD and PENT and we got the first RL. We lost the first RL to the QUAD but we managed to boomstick the RL to death before it got to RA. Control managed to go back and forward for the first five minutes. The score was 30-25 for us at the 15minute PENT. We had secured the PENT + WINDOW with 3 RLs few seconds before it spawned, however we forgot to check the lifts and payed for this dearly as Jester jumped down last second and stole the PENT with perfect timing. The game was very close all the way to the final minute with both teams switching the lead by small margins. The game climaxed with 30 seconds left. We had all 4 at RA with 2 RLs, while all the FAPPERS were at the YA with 2 RLs. There was a large battle for the final QUAD which Interceptor grabbed, but bored seconds later on an enemy boomsticker. Yogic with our other RL attempted to save the pack but was killed from behind with another FAPPERS RL. The final seconds were decided by a few enigmatic boomstick battles. - by American Gangsters

CMT4 - Andromeda Nine (CMT Version)



90-203

Reports
Initial spawns did not go our way as FAPPERS grabbed the first 2RLs YA MEGA RA and PENT, nearly obtaining a full start. We got the first QUAD, but managed to do very little with it. FAPPERS were looking fairly good in the first 5 minutes, however their lack of experience on this map did not help them sustain control. We controlled the majority of the remaining ARMORS and ran some QUADs and PENTs in between cruising to victory. - by American Gangsters

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



148-179

Reports
Once again FAPPERS were treated with the traditional Finnish spawns, getting both the LOWER RL and UPPER RL while we killed off the first QUAD. We fell back to the RA/MEGA area and attacked the second and third QUADs of the game unsuccessfully, giving FAPPERS full lock for the first 3 or so minutes of the game. Interceptor managed to break the lock by sneaking through the TELE defense and stealing the upper RL with which he single handidly took the 4th QUAD of the game and cleared out the WATER/TELE area of enemy RLS. From this effort, we obtained enough RLs to control the TELE and ran the 5th QUAD of the game to LOWER RL clearing out all enemy RLS. We held full level lock for a couple of minutes before the LOW RL fell to the enemy. Yogic was the backbone of the team holding the TELE byhimself for the remainder of the game. We controlled the TELE / UPPER-RL and ran QUADS to LOW-RL never fully securing it, however eliminating any real counter-attack threat for the TELE side until the 17 minute mark. FAPPERS took full control of the level and ran QUADS coast to coast clearing us out. At this point we had over 80 frags lead with 2 minutes remaining in the game and decided to hide and slow-spawn to ensure victory. Well played by FAPPERS. - by American Gangsters
