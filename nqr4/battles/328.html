Battle Info  2003-03-20 Division 3 - Week 2 (2)
 

 
Maniacs 0 - 2 EuthanasiA
 
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



151-168

Reports
here we show why we are in div3 and not in div4 ;) smala making nice quadruns and we had the map the first 12 min but then lost positions when hixen started to blast our asses with his div1 quad skillz ;) Nice hix and GL in div2 next season ;))) /Sassa - by Maniacs

This was Maniacs map of choice and we knew from the beginning that we had to play very sharp and stay alert, cause we knew this team are VERY good on this map. That we became aware of quickly as they ran off with a flying start and it didn't take them long to have a comfortable lead with about 30 frags +/- 10. But after about 12 minutes, one of our guys (it certainly wasn't me, R2D2) got their quad and probably smashed out all of their RL's and we came back strong and took the lead with about 5 minutes of the game. The danger was never really over though, as they flooded Quad and YA frequently, but didn't get the hang of the quad. A very good game by both teams. GG [0] - by EuthanasiA

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



48-232

Reports
their map and with their old star players you cant loose vs maniacs on dm3 ;:) ani (forsakens) hixen (forsakens) r2d2 (fatcat) vortex (dont know ;)) anyways we had forsakens and fatcat in div1 last year and its still a mistery (good english) why \E/ is in div3 and not in div1/2 - by Maniacs

This was our map of choice and we didn't really know how good Maniacs were on this map. But we got off to a pretty good start, getting both Quad and Pentagram if I remeber it correctly, and it didn't take long until we had a big lead. Maniacs didn't really have any chance on this map. They had some quads and gave the RA area a bit of a headache from time to time, but they were never able to get the control of a certain area. They fought well though and they never gave up, which is great, because some clans tend to lame out when they see the score's running away. Good game overall, good luck to Maniacs in the future! - by EuthanasiA
