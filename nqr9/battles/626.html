osams 3 - 2 Wunderbar
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



230-118

Reports
(gaz) steppa got first RL and refused to die for what seemed like a week. His hot action with quad and at RA enabled us to maintain control of the map pretty much all the way. We played well from the word go and only missed the last pent. - by osams

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



187-200

Reports
(gaz) wund got both RLs and quad died, and wund proceeded to hold control for a little while. However, as was the pattern of both DM2s, we didn't let them settle and soon we were right back in it. We managed to sneak a small lead, but wund took over again and it wasn't until about 5 or 6 mins left that we got our noses back into the private parts of wund's DM2 game. We started to claw back the lead, and took the lead with about 2 minutes to go and took the penultimate quad to low... where the ceiling fell in and wund used the RL guys that survived the carnage to take over the map and frag their way in front in the last minute. - by osams

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



240-168

Reports
(gaz) We took over YA fairly early and it stayed like that for most of the game. Like our semi-final vs Epic, our teamplay was too good for our opponents and a superb individual performance from Kauka meant that by the time Wund took over the map with a few minutes to go, we were about 90 frags in front. - by osams

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



177-191

Reports
(gaz) mille got high RL & quad, kauka got low RL and got splatted by mille at water. Wund again took over for a minute or so, but we didn't take long to get back into it. The game bounced back and forward for a while, but after around 10 minutes we really got our teeth into them. We made a lead of around 50, but with about 5 or 6 minutes left, Wund took over and got maplock thanks to good quadrunning from Mille and RA-mega camping by Molecule. They overtook our frag count with about 50 seconds to go, leaving the decider DM3 to go...! - by osams

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



219-108

Reports
(gaz) dopas got RL and molecule got pent. However, he didn't reckon with me, who got quad/ring and then SSG. I managed to bounce him around at YA, stole 2nd YA and then steppa was able to steal 2nd RL, which he kindly bored to me. I then did a decent job of running quad & RA for a few minutes, before wund snuck into RA and I bored with quad at RA-low :D However, their control didn't last long and once again our teamplay and routine was too good for them. Steppa was awesome as he was the first time around whilst Benny did what Benny does. Finally, I'd like to say gg to Wund, it was really fun to play in and spectate, and they were great sports all the way through. Let's hope we meet again in the EQL playoffs! <3 ggs! - by osams
