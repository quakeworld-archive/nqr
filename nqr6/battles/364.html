Battle Info  2004-03-01 Division 2 - Week 4 (4)
 

 
Satanic Slaughter Clan 0 - 2 Griffins Tappra Gossar
 
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



142-243

Reports
First RL and Quad went into SSC paws, while our YA guy picked up first 150 and GL, and used it well to turn the SSC quadder into a red paste. Second RL went in our favor, with YA to boost, and we had our people in place well in time for second quad. We kept going and secured ya/gl/quad area and held on (though barely). SSC tried to bindrush our GL guys but forgot to pickup every RL and so lost their control almost as soon as they took it over. They got a few quads but in general this game was in our hands for nearly all 20 mins. GG! /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar

CMT4 - Andromeda 9



132-146

Reports
ggs! // oosi - by NQR Admin

Most exciting game I've ever had the pleasure to play on this map. We got the first two pents and SSC got the last two. We probably got 10 quads each as well - the game was that tight. After 12 minutes SSC had a good grip on the map, they had 2 shaft/rl/ra guys at water and managed to actually hold it for a while. Then cmt4 did what cmt4 does; punishes the ones who take quad too lightly. A GTG shafter managed to glue their YA-RL who came for quad and break SSCs impeccable control of the map by stealing the 5 minute quad. As I have already told you however, they got the last pent, and with it they managed to take our quadrunner out - but only after he had taken care of another nmy RL or two. We had broken their control and now it was a king of the hill competition at the quad. SSC had a few frags lead with 3-4 mins left, but we proved the more organized at quad, getting all last 3 quads and securing the game in our favor. GGs SSC! /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar
