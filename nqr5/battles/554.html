DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



166-168

Reports
A thriller. We hade a lead of some 50 frags when less than 5 minutes were remaining. Then they cleared the map of our control with one single quad. It was rampage I think :( After this we had no idea what to do - should we try to take control back or start hiding? And what happened? I'm not sure. We struggled, in vain, for a couple of minutes before realizing that they're not gonna lose the control anymore. Then onto the full scale hiding. And once again, the round lasted a bit too long. 10 seconds remaining and the game was drawn. You know the rest. Frag difference +135 in our favor in all 3 rounds combined. And a defeat. ggs / JKova - by El?intarha
