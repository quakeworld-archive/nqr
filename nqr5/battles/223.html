Battle Info  2003-10-28 Division 1 - Week 5 (4)
 

 
Qandrane 0 - 2 The Axemen
 
 

E1M5 - Gloom Keep



150-180

Reports
Uh we knew axemen would choose this map..what a suprise its anyway? well fearsome link was playing..no change for us then..they took control of ya-sng from the start and camped it pretty well..we got few quads and rls but didnt manage to get solid grip of the map..suddenly 3 of their players started to lag (100pl) it lasted about 30-60secs meanwhile janozh holded ya-sng with hes rocket alone..we managed to took over little later but lost control when it was about 5mins remaining..GG - by Qandrane

The idle clan oeks faced the mighty fins from qandrane. But oeks did a tactical move and let Link play, despite his 30 pl, this proved to work well, as qandrane's feared e1m5 skill was making the axemen very very nervous! Sometime mid-game the 3 norwegians lagged out with 100% pl and our only swede had to save the day, by playing cs-flood-o-rama at sng/ya. Qandrane catched up with the frags 5 minutes before the end or so, but oeks' routine on this map and an incredible skilled Link saved the day! A very gg by qandrane though, they knew the map pretty well and played diciplined, forcing oeks to play at their very best with their top e1m5 line up. GG Qandrane! A little tip, dont MSG admins to confirm games; McBilly: hhahaha 18 frags :EEEEEEEEE! - by The Axemen

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



122-123

Reports
axe got pent qand q ring and rl..rl runned to ra and got it..then went to kill axe pent guy but didnt success so well and died shortly after..axe took control of ra and runned few following quads forcing us to ya side..game was pretty tight all the time positions shifting all the time..axe played smart and owned our lonely ya rls with shaft+rl attacks...qand got last pent and for some time it looked bright for us but then again we fucked it up as they killed ya guy and got packs and overtook q..it was janoz who was only guy with weapon at 30sec remaining mark and with q he made enough frags to secure victory..GG again.. - by Qandrane

I didnt get to spec this map in the start, but when I got inn it was very even (read fix report). Both teams followed each other all the way, no team got a good grip. RA control changed many times, and YA too. So it was that kind of dm3 game where anything could happen. Oeks got the 10 min penta but couldnt get heavy control. When Qandrane got the last penta I got a little nervous, oeks had like a 10 frag lead or so but that was eaten up. With 1 minute to go Qandrane was leading 119-116, but oeks got the last quad with a boosted Janozh, who had ra, lg and rl. That last quad was so decisive, and I screamed my team to get it: GET THAT LAST QUAD OR LOOSE THIS MAP! Luckely we got it and CSed the last 15 seconds to not give away any frags. Unlucky for the brave fins :/ but GG anyway Qandrane, nice to see some good upcoming clans in div.1! - by The Axemen
