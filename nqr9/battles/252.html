osams 2 - 0 Shadow Minions
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



261-78

Reports
SM got bored with waiting for MM to finish his shower so we started without him. SM got RL & pent, Benny got Quad/ring. Somehow Benny managed to steal 2nd RL from under the pent's nose and then escaped to RA. Anyway, it was pretty plain sailing for most of the game. Once or twice SM stole a quad and managed some RA takeovers but never really threatened our dynamic duo of steppa/benny. - by osams

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



185-127

Reports
DM2 was SM's choice, and clearly MM had finished washing his enormous penis so he came in for Clox, and we did a mini-line change as well, with steppa and I dropping for tyrone and lakso. Tyrone got low, but the worst happened for us, and MM got tele + high + quad. He fragged us for a few minutes, but after a while we were able to make a takeover and we played pretty well, with Kauka and Lakso helping Benny with the quad running, and Tyrone parking his caravan in the kurwahole. SM managed to take some quads and always had something in the tank at low/ra-mega, but they couldn't break our back. GGs SM, kram! <3 - by osams
