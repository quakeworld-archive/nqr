Battle Info  2003-03-18 Division 5 - Week 5 (2)
 

 
Swedish Super Cops 2 - 1 Civil Disobedience
 
 

E3M3 - The Tomb of Terror



325-18

Reports
The Swedish Super Cops had to turn out this night because we heard rumours about people doing civil disobidience in the town. The cops organized themselves quickly and brought many of them down to our station (e3m3). The Tomb of Terror was full this night and the people who performed civil disobidience where arrested as you can see on the screenshot. In the police report it doesn't state much more than that. SSc managed to keep the whole map for some minutes and never lost RA/RL. - by Swedish Super Cops

E2M2 - The Ogre Citadel



89-181

Reports
However, the proof was not enough against the people performing civil disobidience so the cops had to let them go. :-( After some struggle the cops got a report that they had moved to their homearena (e2m2). This time the Cops didn't stand a chance.. Civil disobidience never got control of the map but they managed to keep the distance and we tried as hard as we could. One reason to why we lost was possibly that one cop didn't know the area so well. But now he does.. :P Very nice played civil disobidience on this map. - by Swedish Super Cops

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



292-123

Reports
Once again it is a 1-1 situation and the famous and mighty ^NQR^ person had to decide where the next demonstration should take place. He decided that the most neutral place would be.. dm2! The cops started out good and got both low rl and quad which some calls full start. But as you know, people performing civil disobidience never gives up. They managed to take some quads and guard the teleport but never managed to take full control. Instead the cops used their quadruns efficiently and locked the map for some minutes and fragged away. The map ended in the swedish super cops favour and another victory for the great cops was a fact. Really good game Civil Disobidience.. no problems with server, players or anything.. nice attitude! Now behave.. :P - by Swedish Super Cops
