     Introduction ^^ 
     

    Nations Quake Rank isn't about money, it's about having fun playing this god given game, QuakeWorld. To be sure all the teams can play each other in a fair environment, we need to have these rules/requirements.

    		 
     
     League system ^^ 
     

    Nations Quake Rank uses a "3-1-0" points system, split over a number of divisions. The divisions are skill-based and so, in theory, the better clans will be in Division 1 and the lesser-skilled clans in the lower divisions.
    Each division, with the exception of Division 1, is split into two groups, called "conferences". These conferences are named A & B; therefore Division 2 is split into 2A and 2B. The clans in these conferences are of equal skill.
    Each clan will play every other clan in their conference once, and only once (during the regular season). All matches during the regular season will be played using the best of 3 system (see below for further details).

    The points system works as follows:

    You win a game that ends: 2-0 = 3 Point(s)
    You win a game that ends: 2-1 = 3 Point(s)
    You loose a game that ends: 1-2 = 1 Point(s)
    You loose a game that ends: 0-2 = 0 Point(s)

    If teams are even on points in the table, the following will seperate the clans (in this order): The map difference, number of maps won and finally the frag difference.

    Each division will have its own playoff competition following the end of the regular season.


    Divisions 3-6:
    The top four clans from each conference will go into the Divisional Playoff competition. The bracket structure will work like this (example given is for Division 3):

    QF1: Clan placed 1st in Div 3A vs Clan placed 4th in Div 3B
    QF2: Clan placed 1st in Div 3B vs Clan placed 4th in Div 3A
    QF3: Clan placed 2nd in Div 3A vs Clan placed 3rd in Div 3B
    QF4: Clan placed 2nd in Div 3B vs Clan placed 3rd in Div 3A

    SF1: Winner of QF1 vs Winner of QF4
    SF2: Winner of QF2 vs Winner of QF3

    FINAL: Winner of SF1 vs Winner of SF2

    Divisions 1 & 2:
    The top six clans will go into the Divisional Playoff competition. The competition will work like this (example given is for Division 1):

    QF1: Clan placed 3rd in Div 1 vs Clan placed 6th in Div 1
    QF2: Clan placed 4th in Div 1 vs Clan placed 5th in Div 1

    SF1: Clan placed 2nd in Div 1 vs Winner of QF1
    SF2: Clan placed 1st in Div 1 vs Winner of QF2

    FINAL: Winner of SF1 vs Winner of SF2

    All divisions:
    Matches in the Quarter-Final stage will be played using best of 3. Matches in the Semi-Final stage and the Final stage will be played using best of 5. For details of map selection, please see below.

    		 
     
     Fakenicking ^^ 
     

    Fakenicking! No fakenicking/aliasing will be tolerated. Please respect the league and the other participants by using your respective/known nicks. If we see this: the FIRST time we will give the CLAN (it doesn't matter who in the clan is guilty, it will be a team warning) a warning.

    The SECOND time this happens, the CLAN (again, it doesn't matter which player in the clan it is that does this, this will be a TEAM PENALTY) in question will receive a points deduction penalty. This deduction will be 1 point to start with and will increase if the clan repeats the offence.

    If a player on the scoreboard is not listed in the members list his frags will ofcourse not be counted. Don't ask admins to add scores of players in your team that were using fakenick during games.

    If a player is thought to be "aliasing/mercing" for a clan (i.e. he is in another clan and not registered for the second clan) then the NQR crew will take a hard line. We will review cases individually but clans should be reminded that anyone caught doing this will face a ban from NQR and heavy points deductions.

    		 
     
     Your team ^^ 
     

    Your team needs to have a working homepage with updated info about all active clanmembers. Teams not fulfilling these requirements can be forbidden to enter the Nations Quake Rank.

    There are no limit to the number of players in one team.

    The following rules apply to changing your team lineup:
    Before the season is "live", you can add and remove players from your roster as you wish.
    After the fixtures are "live" and the divisions have been finalised, all player additions are subject to approval by the admin team. All additions require the approval of AT LEAST two admins so it may take longer than expected to get a player confirmed on your roster; don't add a player 5 minutes before a game because you never know if he will be confirmed or not.

    After week 1 has started, you are allowed to make a maximum of 2 additions to your playerlist during the regular season (subject to approval by the admin team - see above) The admin team reserves the right to deny any player transfer, the crew's decision is final. We will not allow a player transfers that compromise the 'level-ness' of a division.

    No player additions will be allowed once the regular season has finished (catch-up week onwards).

    Players can only transfer between clans during the first 3 weeks of the regular season. After the end of week 3, only players who have not played a map so far in that season can join another clan.

    All teams are expected to behave properly and treat the opponents and admins with respect. Teams can be removed from the league for repeated offences and teams with a known history of trouble can be denied participation.

    		 
     
     Cheating ^^ 
     

    Things that are considered as as cheating:

    Movement scripts of ANY kind. This includes rocket jump scripts.

    Automated teamsays, including automatic mega timer and "autotook" reporting.

    Scripts that change a player's skin depending on their health/armour/weapon.

    Changed models of any kind! Your proxy must report clean set of models to an f_modified check, without exception.

    Hacked clients! Recompiled quake-executables or edited clients will not be tolerated.

    Speedcheating! Achieving a speed percent of over 99.99% is considered as cheating. Since this can happen by accident people who are caught with a higher value will only be asked to reboot and reconnect.

    Teams playing from the same location with two players or more (LAN) are required to notify the admin. The admin can impose restrictions on spectators of that team (to prevent them from relaying the enemys position). Teams having players communicating through other means then in game features or team messages (message mode 2) are required to notify the admin. This includes the use of the voice communication capabilities of Qizmo or a similar or other methods. Only the players currently playing the game are allowed in the voice channel (Qizmo, TS, Vent)! The admin can impose restrictions on spectators of that team.

    No coaching whatsoever from inside the game is allowed, such as; timing quad, relaying enemy postions. This can lead to the player and/or the clan reciving harsh penalties such as a timed kick/ban or a permant kick/ban from the NQR League.

    Other offences

    Intentionally dropping from server! Having one or several players intentionally drop from server during a game without replacing them in no more than one minutes time can be deemed an offence by the preciding admin (depending on circumstances).

    Pausing the game is NOT allowed no matter what the reason's behind it. Any way of interfering with the connection of the opposing team (e.g. by any type of flooding or crashing servers) is obviously prohibited. We reserve the right to treat any behaviour that is deemed to be unsporting as an offence and will be dealt with appropriately, at the discression of the admins.

    Playing in more than one clan, or playing under someone else's name in another clan (mercing).

    If any clan or player is caught doing any of the above they will be punished at the crew's discretion. This could be a warning, points deduction or a ban from NQR.

    Please remember what the game is about, having a good time. Quake is a gentlemans sport you know!

     Match settings ^^ 
     

    Team: 4on4 (This is the prefered nr of players, 3-4 works but should be avoided if possible. The other clan has the choice to either play 4-3 or 3-3. Sportsmanship's unwritten rule however is that you play 3-3)

    Timelimit: 20 minutes
    Overtime: 5 minutes if match is a draw (Should allways be set before game start)
    Deathmatch mode: 1
    Team play mode: 2
    Spawn model: Kteam, KteamPro or Normal QW
    Powerups: ON
    Discharge: ON
    FPS: A max of 77 fps is allowed.
    KFjump: Toggled OFF (command = tkfjump)

    		 
     
     Record demo ^^ 
     

    Since NQR doesn't require an admin to be present at every game, it's very important that you ALWAYS record a demo when your clan plays a game. If there should be any disputes between the clans or anything else concerning the game, you need to show a demo to prove your point.

    		 
     
     Approved maps ^^ 
     

    Division 1:
    [DM2 DM3 E1M2]

    Best of 3:
    Each team chooses a map. If the scores are 1-1 after the maps have been played, the 3rd map from the pool is used as the decider map. The exception to this is that if both teams chose the same map (e.g. 2 x DM3), the decider map will be that map as well.

    Best of 5:
    All 3 maps are played. If the score is 2-1, the team with 1 chooses the 4th map. If a 5th map is required, the other team chooses the final map.


    Divisions 2-6:
    [CMT3 DM2 DM3 E1M2 E2M2]

    Best of 3:
    If the result is 1-1 after two maps have been played, a decider map will be required. If the same map has been played both first maps the decider will be that map as well. Else a map will be agreed upon between the teams, or selected by the throw away system (a team is chosen randomly to throw first map). Example: team1 picks E2M2, teams2 picks DM3 - a decider map will be picked from DM2 E1M2 CMT3. Team1 is chosen randomly to throw away first map. Team1 throws away DM2, team2 throws away E1M2, CMT3 will be played as decider.

    Best of 5:
    Both teams select a map each. After 2 maps have been played, if the score is 1-1, the team that chose their map 2nd must choose the 3rd map. The other team will then choose the 4th map, and the remaining map from the pool will be used as the decider. If the score is 2-0 after 2 maps, then the team losing 2-0 will choose the 3rd map. If a 4th map is required, the team winning 2-1 will choose it.

    		 
     
     Scheduling matches ^^ 
     

    The Nations Quake Rank Crew will pre-schedule all matches. Your clan will typically have 1 scheduled game, and in some cases 2 games, per week. Matches will not be scheduled on a certain day or time, just a certain week, so within this week you must play your scheduled match(es). The exact date and time of the match must be agreed between the two clans involved. However you are allowed to play more games per week if you can, we encourage clans to play more games per week!

    To cater for exceptional cirumstances where teams are for some reason unable to play 2 games per week, a week of contigency is allowed. This means that a game can be delayed by (a maximum of) one week. However, if a game has still not been reported after this additional week, it will be locked out and it will be impossible to report the game.
    This game can be unlocked again in future if the game has been played.

    Obviously we don't like to see Walkover or Void games, so at the end of the regular season there will be a single week of "catch-up" games where the reporting system will be opened in order to allow any games that could not be played to be completed. However, please note the following important rules:

    1. No Walkover results can be submitted during this week (other than for week 7 games - see below rules on WOs)
    2. Don't rely on this week! You are obliged to play your games during the period that they are reportable. If Clan A wants to play Clan B in the alloted timeframe, Clan B cannot refuse and assume that they can play the game in this final "catch-up" week - Clan A will be within their rights to report a Walkover win! The "catch-up" week is for games that *both* clans failed to arrange.

    Additionally, the NQR admin team will consider moving fixtures to another week provided that the clan(s) have a good reason and that they give plenty of notice! This will be done entirely at the admin teams' discretion.

    About Walkover Games

    Teams that submit a walkover should be able to provide some kind of proof that they actively attempted to arrange the game. Walkovers are used as a *last resort only*! Clans must use the entire 2-week period to attempt to schedule a game. If the first attempt fails, try and reschedule before considering a Walkover result.
    If you wish to report a W.O., you must do so at the end of the week AFTER the scheduled week. So, for example for a week 4 game you must report the W.O. at the end of week 5.

    Once a game has been voided a W.O. cannot be accepted.

    		 
     
     Inactivity ^^ 
     

    Inactivity will not be tolerated. NQR is for active clans, not idlers. The NQR admin team reserves the right to remove any team from the league that they consider to be idling. You have been warned!

    		 
     
     Server issue ^^ 
     

    The matchserver is decided by both teams. A server giving approximately equal connection conditions should be found. In principle, all maps are played on the same server.

    If a fair server cannot be found, cmd minping can be set to bring the AVERAGE ping of the lower pinging team to the same as the opposition, upto a maxmimum value of 45 (unless agreed otherwise).

    If teams cannot agree on a server conditions then an NQR Admin should be asked to settle the conflict. The NQR Admin's decision is final!

    		 
     
     Proxies and clients ^^ 
     

    The only proxys/clients allowed in NQR are Mqwcl 0.96 and the latest official release versions of FuhQuake(currently 0.31, build 675). The Qizmo proxy version 2.91 is also allowed as it can help with routing. Standard QWCL clients are no longer allowed. During regulear season the NQR Crew will often NOT be on a server to check for cheat's etc. So it is up to YOU players to check eachothers settings. We press on this issue to be done before any game, and after each map. NQR Crew will allways attend during NQR Playoffs, and will then peform the checks needed to be done. But as stated below, if argument should happen between 2 clans, then contact an admin to settle the conflict, what the admins says and decides is not negitionable.

    Concerning FuhQuake/Mqwcl f_checks

    FuhQuake is required to have -ruleset smackdown and -norjscripts cmd line parameter added.

    To check for cheats, such as fake clients, modified models and sound, you need to check 2 things. First you have to determin, wether or not the clients on the server are genuine, ie. not modified. Next you can check for modified models. The procedure is explained in detail as follows:

    1. Checking for client authentication:
    For Mqwcl you need to do an f_version, then you need to do an f_version, then a /list_clients (remember the "list_clients command ONLY works with mqwcl client) to check if the replying Mqwcl clients are genuine.

    For FuhQuake you need to do an f_version, then a /validate_clients to check if the replying FuhQuake clients are genuine.

    2. Checking for modified models, sounds etc:
    f_modified will reply if players have modified anything. All players must reply all models ok.

    If a player drops after a f_check, the player must be rechecked when he returns to the server. If an argument should occur between the clans, then the clans should go to #NQR on IRC and get an admin to come to the server and settle the conflict. Any player/clan that shows some bad models or other bad settings must change them at once, failing to do so and an admin may impose restrictions upon this player/clan, such as giving the other clan a WO or banning the clan/player from NQR.

    NOTE: There is a bug when routing via Qizmo proxies. It will post "modified player/eyes model" when you enter a server. Don't mind this, as long as f_version shows an original authenticated client, and f_modified shows all ok.


    NOTE: EZQuake is not allowed in NQR7. We will be reviewing the situation at the end of the season with a view to seeing if it should be allowed during NQR8 and beyond.

    		 
     
     Reporting a game ^^ 
     

    As soon as a game have been played, the winning team should report the result to NQR. To report a game you simply login on the NQR site and choose the necessary option.

    NOTE: If you report a game that weren't played the same day you MUST notify the NQR Crew about that.

    If by some reason this don't work you can send an e-mail to nationsquakerank@gmail.com containing:

    1. The Name of the clans that were playing.
    2. What maps that were played and the result for each clan.
    3. Attach screenshots in 320x200 pixels GIF or JPG format for each map.
    4. The Date the game was played.

    If you have any problems reporting games, just contact the admins on #nqr and we will help you out.
