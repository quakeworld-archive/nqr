  IMPORTANT! Members and Homepage!  030929  
 

Some clans have still yet to add members to their clan, we STRESS the importance of this now, and give you 1 last chance to add your members to your team.

What is also essential is an working homepage, doesn't have to be anything fancy, just a updated member list with contact info.

Clans who has not complied before 24.00 CET Tuesday, will be deleted! If your clan's homepage is under construction plz notify the admins, and atleast add your members to NQR page!

Taken from rules page "Your Team":

"Your team needs to have a working homepage with updated info about all active clanmembers. There are no limit to the number of players in one team. Teams not fulfilling theese requirements can be forbidden to enter the Nations Quake Rank."

Also players registered with a different nick then they use to play with, should have this marked on their member page on NQR, for example; Leg (Legio!) <- beeing the nick you play with.

Playoffs

Playoffs will be a bit different from last season, this season the 8 best clans from Division 1 will advance to playoffs. Due to time shortage, and other issue's there will be no "mini-playoff / relegation games" for the lower divisions. This is sad, but not much we can do about that now. We will have some kind of "carrot" for you players/clans in the lower divisions next season.

If you have any question's regarding anything you are more then welcome to contact an admin at #NQR.
