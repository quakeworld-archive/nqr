Battle Info  2005-03-25 2 - Week 4 (3)
 

 
Satanic Slaughter Clan 2 - 0 4Honor
 
 

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



219-145

Reports
I was already in my bed reading Linux Journal when phone rang several times and I was like "wtf now". Seemed that we had a NQR match, so I got up. 4Honor choosed e1m2, which first seemed like a "oh no" choice for us first, but after few minutes of play we got a feeling that we might actually win this. We managed to keep ya and get most of the quads. Seni mostly harrashed rl nmy's and reported their locations. I think it went pretty well. - by Satanic Slaughter Clan

CMT3 - Deutschmaschine



192-116

Reports
We choosed cmt3 as we guessed that 4Honor wasn't so good on kenyas. We got a fullstart with pent/rl/ring and nmy quad was killed quite soon. We mostly held ra and tried to kill nmy rl's with quad. I think we got all pents. My personal contribution was kinda weak and mostly just whined about the the ping/pl in TeamSpeak. Seems like Milton was the dominator. - by Satanic Slaughter Clan
