Battle Info  2003-04-13 Division 3 - Week 5 (5)
 

 
EuthanasiA 2 - 0 FUDOH
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



234-84

Reports
Before the game we had long discussions on which server to play on, and after convincing a pretty annoyed Dexter that he (once again) had to play with 2x his usual ping, we agreed to play on a norwegian server. Though that hardly mattered on this map as we got a steady lead early on and it wasn't to much of a problem to hold and secure the map, though they did break through to RA sometimes - but- as you probably can see by the score, Hixen pretty much owned them with his berzerk quadruns. Solid play overall. GG - by EuthanasiA


Demos
-


  
 

E3M7 - The Haunted Halls



131-128

Reports
This was a pretty strange game. Hixen warned us before the start of the game that we should be aware of Stalin on this map, and, what can you say? He stood for ~50% of their frags and - not to go down on any of the other FUDOH players, they played great - it felt like Stalin probably could have played this map alone. I have seldom seen such a domination from one player. BUT as you can see, that didn't help. After about 12-13 minutes Vortex somehow managed to do this unbelievable quadrun and we took over the whole RA/Pentagram section, and due to solid play by everyone in EuthanasiA, we were able to gain a lead with about 10 frags and held that 'til the end. They took over RA/Pentagram with about 1 minute left, but that wasn't enough time to comeback, and we managed to pull this through with as little margin as possible. - by EuthanasiA
