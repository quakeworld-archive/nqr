I was away at a lan i helped arrange last week, and when i got back i slept for two days, apparently i had forgot to give the password to report games to anyone else in the clan, so they couldn't do it themselves, and then when i was finally online and had got the screenshots and everything, it was no longer possible to report that game, so i talked to admins and sent a mail about it, then it took a few days before they had time to fix it...

I wasn't there when they played the game, so I'll just write what i've been told about it:
One of fragunits guys couldn't play, and both clans agreed to do it 3on3. First map was dm2 (FU's choice), it was an even game, and FU won by a small margin, then they played our choice map e3m6, which was apparently an even closer game, ending in a TIE, yes, a tie. The server they played on was apparently not configured for overtime, or even sudden death... so... since neither clan wanted to replay the map, they decided to play dm2 a second time - our guys felt they could beat FU, and being FU's home map, i guess they felt the same - this time around though, our guys managed to get a somewhat better control of the game, and won by a comfortable margin. The last map played was e1m2, which we are supposed to be pretty good at, statistically speaking, at least... it turned out to be one of our good e1m2-days, and we returned triumphantly here too.

All in all it seems like a Good Game?, and i wish i'd been there to see it (or even play)
