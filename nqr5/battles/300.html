Battle Info  2003-10-13 Division 2 - Week 1 (3)
 

 
American Gangsters 2 - 0 Fired
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



191-122

Reports
We got both the first UPPER and LOWER RLs of the game while Fired built a quick ladder to the first QUAD. Our first UPPER RL made short work of the enemy QUAD, while the LOWER RL immidiately locked the TELE. Within a minute of the game we enjoyed a full lock on the level, however our enthusiastic start blurred our priorities. We retreated from the LOWER RL area, giving 2 RLs to Fired which came to haunt us a minute later at the next QUAD. Fired pulled off a by-the-book attack on QUAD which helped them slowly finnish off our RLs. The mid point of the game saw FIRED holding on strongly to the TELE side and running QUAD to the LOWER-RL smashing our counter-attack potential. Our attacks on the QUAD were uncoordinated, our RA-MEGA defense was holding on partially, and our score lead was diminishing at a drastic pace. Things were not looking good until the final few minutes of the game at which point we finally managed to coordinate and retake the QUAD, clear out the TELE, and hold on to it for the remainder of the game which gave us the victory margin. Fired showed off its true colors on this map, while its back to the drawing board for us on DM2 strat. - by American Gangsters


Demos
def([AG])

 
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



285-86

Reports
Initial spawns saw Fired take the first RL MEGA GA while we obtained the first YA GL QUAD. Fortunately for us, we've made better use of our starting resources (especially the GL) and secured the YA in the first few minutes of the game without any RLs. Fired managed to kill off the first two QUADs with coordinated rushes at the expense of many frags. At this point we obtained enough RLs to safely control YA GL and MEGA areas. Consequent QUAD runs penetrated Fired's front line all the way into the catacombs of E1M2. Fired managed to retake the YA over the course of the game a few times, only to get flushed seconds later with our QUAD. GGs - by American Gangsters
