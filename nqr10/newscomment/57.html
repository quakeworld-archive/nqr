Hey guys. The groups have been set up and you can now start playing your games!
Keep in mind that you will have to play quite a few teams stronger or weaker than yourselves. For some clans it will obviously be quite tough, but on the other hand you could see this as a great experience and a worthy challenge. And as we all know, the best way to improve is to play a superior clan.

Having said that, we wish you all good luck! If there remain any questions or problems, don't hesitate to contact us on #nqr on Quakenet.

The map-pools are as follows:

Gold: TB3 Only
Silver: TB3 Only
Bronze: TB3 Only

Fakeshaft is allowed in all of the divs.

djevulsk - 85.24.189.*** 070209 @ 12:56:55
wow, i can write a whole essay on this subject but i'll try to keep it short (and note that i speak for myself, more than a nqr admin this time):

some teams will always be misplaced, even if (or especially if) there are more divisions. look at every season of nqr/eql, or just eql4 for that matter. some clans will only win 1 single map and some clans will go undefeated, that's just the way it is.

one of the reasons of merging divisions is simply because we, for example, don't have 10 or so div1 teams - we have 5ish. a truly even division of 5 is not much fun, is it? by splitting them up in two groups, the not-div1-clans in gold (including my own) will have 2-3 impossible games, but is that really that bad? your suggestion div1 + top div2 has been tried and doesn't really work since the skill difference is huge. i believe (who knows before it's been tried?) that merging will be good for the scene if it somehow get clans to play clans they don't normally play (instead of refusing which imho leads to inactivity). i chose div1 as an example as it is familiar to everyone, but the same goes for practically all divisions.

there's MANY reasons to care about the size of groups. here's a few:
evenly sized groups are extremely important as most teams should have, theoretically, the same chance of reaching playoffs. top4 playoffs in a group of say 13 is a lot different than in a group of 9. I know there's many ways to deal with this, but we've chosen 3 weeks of playoffs which means quarter-semi-finals (that means 8 teams in playoffs).

another reason is that a season shouldn't be to long since clans disbandon, go inactive etc quite often. 8 weeks + playoffs as it is now is quite long and managing 12 games (groups of 13 as you suggest) in 8 weeks is really pushing it for some clans.

also, we feel the need try something new instead of eql's system of div1-6 (or nqr's, you know what came first). hopefully players can try to stay open minded and atleast try it out. if it doesn't work out i can guarantee i (and everyone else involved) will do their best to improve both the placement of clans and the structure of the tournament.

i'm not really interested in a long discussion here so this will basically be it for me (if i haven't forgot something vital). i hope we can focus on playing our games (and enjoying ourselves while doing it). there'll be plenty of time for discussions afterwards. then we also know what didn't turn out so good instead of assuming everything sucks in advance.

of course the league is for players. claiming that we do this for our own sake is quite ridicilous imho. if you feel however that you have great ideas and a lot of time to give to the scene, feel free to apply as a new admin. we need you!
