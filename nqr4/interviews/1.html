Interviews  

  Slurpas  2003-03-13  
I thought a long time that i was going to interview some big hotshot in Division1, but i changed my mind and choosed to let a not so known player from Division6 speak out and make his thoughts heard. So i went on to his clan channel #skk and started to priv chat with him and asking him some questions. This is what got out from him that magic night in mars.
 
1. Real Name, City, Age & Played Quake for how long?
Name: Martin G?rdrup
From: Sweden
City: L?ddek?pinge, S?dra Sandby, and Lund

Played Quake for how long:
i dont know how long. I did start to play Quake like 96 or 97 i think.
But i havent been active all the time. I am a Hardcore gamer, so i play
like 4-6 games "at the same time".
I did get active again for some weeks ago, and think that my q3/cs/ut aim and skills shall help me in qw.
 
2. How long have SKK existed? Is it all new members now or oldies?
i dont know, i did join up like 2 weeks ago. SKK have existed since 1996, but have been inactive for e while. The most of the members are new, but the clanleader row have been here for a while.
He was one of the 3 that did start SKK.
 
3. You start out in Div6 in NQR4, how does it feel to start in a Division so far from Div1? What are your expectations for the season? Got any special goals for the clan in upcomming seasons of NQR?
It feels fine just to start playing again. I like Shoot em up games, and
im happy just to play in any leauge. I dont need to be in Div1 because, im just playing 4 fun, but it would be nice to play in some higher division.
 
4. You have allready played HOT and the Div6 leading clan Thangorodrim, you lost both. What clan will you try to bring the pain to this time? Got a date set?
We shall play against FNU before 18th mars. I think that it will be on
friday.
 
5. People have been gossipin' about clan Thangorodrim being to good for Div6, what is your view on that?
Hehe, u must start at some place and then work the clan up in the
divisions. I dont know much about Thangorodrim
but as an "old gamer", i know that its not a good tactic just to
take some good players and make a clan.
If u shall be good u must like eachother even outside qw and it is a good

start to have LAN with the persons in ur clan.
 
6. Overall, what is your expectation on NQR? Do you like the system? The maps?
I like the system and the maps, i have no more to add.
 
7. Do you got some grandparents you wanna say HI! to? this is the time.
I just wanna say GL to everyone out there, i love to play and im glad that so many other people have the same intrest.
(ps thanks to all people that have played with me and thx to my pals from S?dra sandby that "help" me to play)
 
8. Thanks for letting me abuse your time.
anytime!
