Battle Info  2007-04-15 Bronze Cup Quarterfinal
 

 
Clan Cube Squad 0 - 2 EnemyQuad
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



38-177

Reports
So we face the winners from group A, Clan Cube Squad. Never played them so didnt know what to expect. They chose DM2 as their map. They get first Q but Igggy made him dead as ioh would say. We got first two RLs and the rest of the quads more or less. Don't know what happened in the CCS camp at low but we didn't see them much over at our side of the pond. - by EnemyQuad

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



133-195

Reports
Home map for EQ and now we were more confident. However CCS came out guns blazing and giving us a run for our money. It was quite close most of the game but in the end we had more or less control of the YA/GL area. This being the quaterfinal it's best of three and with this mapwin it was a done deal. Advancement for EQ. Well played team and GGs CCS. - by EnemyQuad
