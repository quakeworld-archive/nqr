Battle Info  2004-10-27 3B - Week 4 (4)
 

 
Osams 1 - 2 Klatch!
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



175-88

Reports
Match report by gaz: DM3 - our map. I got penta and ponk got RL. I think Klatch got quad but I'm not sure. Anyway I made a nice trick that I learned from Goljat in SR-fOm in NQR6 and stood on the YA tele exit in case their RL guy went through... and he did. Wohoo.. I took second RL and made my way via pent and SNG (tried to get quad but they were all there with sng etc) and secured red. We didn't lose control of the big shiny red one until about 4 minutes to go when Klatch attacked and killed the guy there. They made some frags back in the last few minutes but our lead was too big thanks to lunna & lovevibration's quad running, gg! - by Osams

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



142-147

Reports
They clearly feared our UK players on CMT4 and so they threw it. Damn we thought, so we threw CMT3. We got the first 3 rocket launchers and slowly built a bit of a lead, but soon Klatch made a nice come back and through their efforts at the dick room, they took over YA and went back into the lead. This game was very close all the way through, and Klatch was leading by 12 frags with 90 seconds to go. We had control at this point, with 2 RLs. We were forced to hunt for frags but couldn't quite make it. Close game, could have gone either way! gg klatch, *pattah* *ponk* *le game* *klatch* :D - by Osams
