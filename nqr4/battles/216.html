Battle Info  2003-03-19 Division 1 - Week 2 (2)
 

 
Firing Squad 2 - 1 The Axemen
 
 

E1M5 - The Gloom Keep



150-200

Reports
We had a good start, and got first rl, sng and quad. We controled rl and sng, and got a 40-50 frags lead. But we gave away some rl's and fs had control over ya+button and gl/ya. They came more into the game and also had control over quad for some time. One quad came to sng, and owned 3 axemen's, but fs never took complete control. The last 6-7 minutes was kinda even, but we gained a little more frags than fs. gg fs. - by The Axemen

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



258-94

Reports
Ev0l start for axemen, fs got first rl, penta, ring, quad and ra. Tco and Tim telefragged eachother on sng. That made both ring and quad kinda lost. We took out a rl on ra I think, but they also had shaft there. Fs pushed hard on the first rl's, but janozh managed to take the second from penta. He also denied a few more rl's, preventing total fs control in the start. Although TiMMi had some few 200 ping boosts, when he had rl causing him to give away 2 rl's before second penta. Fs had control over ra, taking most quad's and making juice out of axemens. We never had a chance on penta, and we were mostly running around as ducks. We had a nice RA takeover late-midgame. But fs came with quad and wiped us out. Not so goood game by us. gg fs. - by The Axemen

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



342-101

Reports
Rl low to fs, quad to fs, rl high to axemen. But our rl high got owned, and fs quad went to rl low. It became a seriously total rape the first minutes. Even if we got second quad and high rl, it went into a huge br0st on quad/high rl. Fs took the grip, and had a guy on ra+mega, tele, quad, everything. Tco stopped, and typed GG and great map, and seconds later, it was 100-10 to fs. +forward was our key, and we just whined about the map in mm2\mm3. Then we found out that tele was a great place to spam, so we actually took over for a minute or two. We gained like 50 frags, and had a VERY small hope. Score was 114-50 I think, when fs regained control. Our problem on dm2 is that when our opponent have control, we give away thousands of frags, while our opponent play smart, when we gain tele+quad. Late game, we took over once or twice, but never took complete control. If we remove the first 5 minutes, this game actually was a little more even than the result shows. gg fs, great map. :) - by The Axemen
