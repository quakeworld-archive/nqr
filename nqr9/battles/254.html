DM3 - The Abandoned Base



207-116

Reports
(gaz) Kauka got RL, one of them got pent, I got ring and steppa got quad. Somehow their pent guy had a brain fart and didn't even get close to 2nd RL, which meant that Steppa took it and secured RA. Then he timed out with RA/RL, and Kauka & I both died with our RL within 20 seconds, giving Gold a chance. However, we didn't let them fuck with us for long, and soon we got our teeth in at RA. They managed a few takeovers and ran some quads, but this was our show. It was quite FFA from us at times, we have played a lot better than this for sure :P - by osams

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



191-130

Reports
1 RL each at the start, quad died, and then the game followed this pattern: we hold tele, take quads, try to clear map. Kip is a pain in the ass and stops us from fully locking down and getting comfortable. Eventually we broke through and this enabled us to pull clear (it was very close up until 9 mins left, maybe less than that). - by osams
