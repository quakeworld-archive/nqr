DM3 - The Abandoned Base



121-122

Reports
LOL @ SPAWNS ----------------------------------------------------------- Most likely the most entertaining game we've ever played. Congrats to our rivalclan osams and good luck in the future - by Team MP

DM3, well, OMG what a game. It started out kind of messy with MP getting the first pent and clox spawning on the RL that the pent was waiting for. We secured RA for a while but MP managed to take it back and since we left a lot of packs we soon found that they all had RL's and good control of the map. In some bizarre way we managed to get some quads and after the 10 minute mark all hell broke loose. Both teams were making mistakes, leaving packs and forgetting the quads and not guarding the key areas. With 2 minutes to go MP was up with a twenty or so lead and things looked really dark for us. One of the MP guys did the genius move and discharged in the water where they were hiding reducing the lead from 5 to 2. After some desperate chasing of the MP people that did their best to hide they had a 1 frag lead with less than 1 second to go. Who died? Well, one of the MP guys (no names :D) that was hiding in the water was killed with less than 0.0001 seconds to go. No one knew who won when the game was over and MP more or less thought that they had the victory but TADA.WAV - osams wins this hilarious match with 1 frag with one nano second to go! Very exciting, 3 more points to us and big hugs to our brothers-in-arms MP. - by osams
