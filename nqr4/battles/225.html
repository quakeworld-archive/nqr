DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



115-157

Reports
a bg from our side.... but this will never happen again! - by Campbusters

CB vs AXE is what you can call a local derby, we were seated next to eachother on qhlan#5, and some of us have visited them in SKAAAAAUNE, so this was a true game of glory. :) And of course, the best team won. 8) CB got the better start, and really got a good grip the first 5-6 minutes. We were like 60-70 frags under, when we attacked quad, and took over. The AXE effect striked CB, and they were forced back to low. After 10-12 minutes, it was back too even again. After that, CB started to cry, as they foresaw the outcome. "PLEASE Dm3 TOO" and stuff like that, appeared on my screen. :) But, axe played great and made it a flawless 2-0 IN FAVOUR NORWAY!!! GG CB! :D - by The Axemen
