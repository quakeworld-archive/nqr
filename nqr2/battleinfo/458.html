  2002-03-18  
Clan MalFunction vs KOFF
     
  DM3  
   
  242 - 86  
 
We started the game with our homemap, dm3. DM3 has been pretty strong for us lately since we have played it quite a lot in the past few weeks. In the start fix got RL and all the powerups went to KOFF. This time fix managed to stay alive with the first rl (infact he stayed alive for 16mins until eh killed him and ruined his 100% eff) and the game started go pretty well for us. We got ra and quad pretty well under control and started to gain a nice lead.. Janne took the second penta and all was fine at the moment.. Everything went on just fine until the second pent, which our guys seemed to forget and KOFF took it. With that pentarun they managed to kill couple our rl guys and we were down to 2 rl guys in the rl side.. KOFF had a chance to make a comeback but they didnt use it, our guys fought bravely and reclaimed the lost positions and guns! We were well prepared for the third pent, delayd the quad for about 20-30secs so that quad guy could grab penta easily, but KOFF guy destroyed our clever plan by dischargin our quadrunner in the penta window. KOFF got the last pent but it was already too late for KOFF to win this round since we had a rathar large lead. Both teams fragged in the 5mins and the game ended with a nice victory for us! Report by mooniz
