Battle Info  2003-11-12 Division 1 - Week 4 (6)
 

 
Four Kings 1 - 2 Slackers
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



75-298

Reports
This was Slackers map choice - I expect they wanted the map where they could boost their fragranks the most ;). But we actually got a good start and were winning for the first 4-5 minutes, taking 90% of the quads in this time. But, inevitably, SR's quality showed through. gg from Serox for us... - by Four Kings

CMT4 - Andromeda Nine (CMT Version)



182-117

Reports
We thought we had a small chance at this map, but tbh I still thought SR were favourites. But we played it really well, holding all the key areas and getting most quads and pents. - by Four Kings

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



71-230

Reports
We agreed on dm3 as a decider and the game went similarly to the dm2 one. We had the better start and took a number of quads at the start. But once SR got both RA and YA the game was over, despite a few spirited fightbacks. Overall, a point against SR is about as much as we hoped for. Big shouts to eriq who came in and played despite not really playing much qw in the last few months. gg m8! - by Four Kings
