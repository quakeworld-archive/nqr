since we're always to lazy to write a matchreport, i'll just go ahead and write a short one in the comments:

cmt4: they really surprised us with their mapchoice, because we thought they would go for dm3 and so we practiced that map all week (gg). cmt held control of the map pretty well up until the 12th minute and we're leading comfortably by about 20-30 frags for most of the time until a few lucky fights turned the game in our favour, in the end we were pretty much able to lock the map, thus the high score in our favour.

cmt3: concluding the 2nd episode of kenya night, we went to cmt3 (our homemap). we felt rather confident in the warmup, but that was soon to change after the 1st minute, since cmt got pent, quad, rl, ra and shaft (also in part due to me fucking up the quad spawn), so again we found ourselves battling a cmt lead. Fortunately we could turn the map rather quickly and gain a more confortable lead up until the 10th minute, where we made some mistakes and they closed in within 15 frags. Some not so effective quadruns by cmt let us come back into the game and so in the end we were able to take cmt3 as well.

ggs cmt and gl in the playoffs, im sure you will reach them.
