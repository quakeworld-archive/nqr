CMT3 - DeutschMaschine



95-64

Reports
Optimizer had to leave because his sister needed the computer, though after about five minutes we decided to play 3on3. Green went to spec and Glad switched to lpb. I got first ra but my keyboard was lagging for 20 minutes (didn't respond at all sometimes and often I just walked/strafed on in 1 direction in which I didn't want to go) so I missed the first quad. Blaze who spawned RL took it and they got control of RA for 8 minutes and ran quads, we got to steal some but those died quickly as we worked from RL/YA. We got a takeover after those 8 minutes when we killed all enemies at once and I was able to clear eveything with the next quad and took pent. Because I got to time RL for some minutes and eriq and mur were doing ra their resistance was low. With five minutes left we were leading with 40 frags and eriq got the last pent which practicaly established our victory even though they got some of the late quads and ra's. Thanks for playing 3on3 in the last round and gg's. Purity - by Dutch Hope
