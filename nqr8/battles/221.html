Battle Info  2005-03-21 1 - Week 5 (2)
 

 
KOFF 0 - 2 Griffins Tappra Gossar
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



177-182

Reports
Koff gets the first pent, while we manage to split quad, ring and rl among three of our players. Riker starts out good with his first RL, mrlame keeps RA/Ring safe for the first 30 with quad, and I manage to steal and neutralize second RL from pent with ring/sng. All was swell until third/fourth quad or so, by which time we've screwed up (of course) and let koff get to RA with RL. By second pent they had a force ready while we were weak and didn't meet much opposition from our assault. Koff led the game from that point on and kept us on the low end. It wasn't until the last few minutes of the game that we managed to sieze control of the map, and with that little time left koff went for the sauna - but it was too early. With 30 sec or so left, we passed em by, and in our first official game of the season we manage to win DM3. Lets hope we can keep up that standard *knocks on wood*. //dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar


Demos
All Pov (MVD)

 
 

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



143-248

Reports
E1M2 was never really up for grabs. Although Koff did a good job keeping their RLs alive (though barely) they hardly ever managed to get them into a dominating position at YA, and when they did, we kept them locked at GL while we swept the quad, preventing them from clearing us out. In the end we got 18 quads vs their 2, and letting that many quads fall into bonde hands is a mistake of historic proportions. Koff fought well however, and managed to keep at least 1-2 of us dead at all times, preventing us from locking mega and keeping them from all the ammo. GGs guys, good luck with the rest of the season. //dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar
