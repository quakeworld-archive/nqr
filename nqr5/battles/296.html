stal (80.202.35.92) 2003-10-17 14:48:19
Hagge: There is no 'zerodamage bug', but rather a '1 point damage bug'. A rocket fired by a player affected by this seemingly random, but far too frequent, e3m7-bug will only do 1 point of damage (nothing to do with handicap) with a rocket :[ I feel really bad for teams loosing maps this way. Just look at http://www.nqr.nu/battles.php?id=350 for another example (fiery on e3m7). If you don't believe me, just message for demo (#fudoh). IBH|123 has taken a look at the demo from that game, and promised to notify rxr. Really nice with people who care about this stuff ! How to avoid the bug: Switch map to e3m7-> all players readyup-> test damage-> reconnect if you don't do damage-> break-> readyup and play the match with no bugs. This has worked in all of fudoh's e3m7 games, and quite a few times we've discovered the bug in this short 'test phase'. However, it's usually hell trying to explain the problem for our opponents, even though it only takes like 1 minute..
