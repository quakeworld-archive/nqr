DM3 - The Abandoned Base



171-125

Reports
Hipsters got first pent but we got quad and BEST POWERUP EVER (ring). Don't really remember who got the first rl but that doesn't really matter because 1 minute into the game something really weird happened. Starplayer Benny Barnyard changed name to Jack Bauer and started spamming shit about terrorists and bombs. 30 seconds later Benny Barnyard was back with no memory of what happened. We didn't really have any control of the map and neither did hip so it was mostly down to individual skills. We manageakghnbka WHERES THE BOMB?!? I HAVE TO STOP THE TERRORISTS! - by osams

not surprisingly, osams got fullstart in their map getting pent, ring, quad, rl and ng. luckily with some mm3 axion we weren't matlocked at any time and lost simply by sucking too much. 2bad, i thought we had a decent chance against the osams b-team without their two biggest stars steppa and lunna. :( - by Hippushnik

220-167

Reports
Hips choice, it was all one big ffa-fragfest. Rocketlaunchers boring and leaving packs all over the place. Insane hip-players chasing you down 4on1 with SNG.
