Battle Info  2006-04-23 Silver Cup Premier - quarter-final
 

 
DQM The Stars 0 - 2 epic
 
 

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



187-192

Reports
All right then. Our first playoff game became reality and we somehow managed to get four people playing. Vert lagged like hell but it didn't stop him from playing. So the game started and Raket got the quad and some other enemy got the rl. After that I think we got rl's pretty equally and had some quads too. I wasn't feeling too well after the last E1M2 I (Renzo) played so I asked some instructions how am I supposed to play. Smala told me to get some stuff and head for the rl after that. So I decided to play like that. Luckily enemies were dropping like flies so I had my time to get few rls and boomsticked the rest of them. I probably didn't do too much for the team but they were hanging there killing enemies in the process. This was a great round and I didn't realize that we actually won the round before I checked scoreboard after the round ended. - by epic


Demos
All Pov (MVD)


  
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



164-184

Reports
So it was our turn to choose the map then. Of course we chose DM2. Our start was pretty bad and vert was still lagging. In the beginning Persu managed to steal the quad but died instantly. From that point on, we kinda played mixed tactics. Not so aggressive attack but not so aggressive defend either. It was pretty tight round after all but somehow persu managed to get the deciding frags while I (Renzo) was getting bored with life at quad (must see from demos :D). Even if vert lagged, he played really smart there on the tele/ya and saved us many times. Pretty chaotic stuff but good games after all. - by epic


Demos
All Pov (MVD)
