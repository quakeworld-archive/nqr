Battle Info  2003-10-05 Division 7 - Week 1 (1)
 

 
F?reningen r?dda igelkottarna 2 - 0 SmackThatAss
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



155-119

Reports
In the beginning both teams played pretty even. Fri took the first quad but sTa killed it right away. After that fri took positions next to the quad while sTa were holding low-rl. After that sTa suddenly took over quad and fri were pushed to the low-rl. sTa took a few quads but then; once again, fri took control. In the end sTa did a pretty nice come back but it was not enough time left. GG. -Werkaster - by F?reningen r?dda igelkottarna

dm2 was our first ever QW (NQR) league match. We started of with stress even before the match began as one of our better players didn't show up (still MIA). Nevertheless we managed to get started eventually. Overall we are quite pleased how the map went down. We managed to get several Quads and get/ keep control of main areas in the map. However F?reningen r?dda igelkottarna (.se) just performed better as we did. And took the deserved victory on this map with them. - by SmackThatAss

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



182-104

Reports
Fri's homemap. Just like dm2 fri took the first quad but died right away (bad quadrun sue ;)). But fri managed to hold the ya/quad-zone most of the time and sTa had a hard time to get there alive. But after 10 minutes or something sTa began to attack more offensive and took the quadruns for a while. But with many rls and high screams fri played pretty good in the end and victory was a fact. GG once again. - Werkaster - by F?reningen r?dda igelkottarna

Our pre season didn't deliver as much practice as we would have wanted. And that cleary showed in the e1m2. Despite being one of the big 3 maps and everyone basicly knowing the map it clearly showed that we lacked tp experience. F?reningen r?dda igelkottarna took control of the map straight from the beginning and we could never give them a real run for their money. - by SmackThatAss
