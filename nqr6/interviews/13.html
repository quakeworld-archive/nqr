Interviews  

  satanic  2004-04-07  
He's the leader of one of the sexiest and most friendly clans in the QW scene right now, and boy is he hot. His name is satanic, but his friends call him sata. His clan, the Pigs of Fucking Fury, fuck furiously towards victory in NQR division 4...
 
1. Sup sata :)
Not much, easter break from school
 
2. Tell me a bit about the sata from real life
My name is Jonas Danielsson, im 21 and studing to become a civic engineer in Lund, sweden
 
3. What do you enjoy doing in your free time besides playing qw?
Hmm, i have a sunday night poker game with my friends that I enjoy, other than that me and expociia (also in poff) play pool all night at the local pub. I also have a girl friend, Helena, her quake2 nick is Sathurday. She wanted to play for poff, but honestly, she's not very good.
 
4. So nothing involving any real pigs?
Mm, no not really :), it was Huli-Man and his irl friends that started the pigs of fucking fury in 1997, I joined up in 1998, before that I played in a clan with some friends, and mostly lans with the QAOS clan (fox, maestro, etc) and jezaja
 
5. What are your goals in poff?
Well, poff has been around a long time now, and it has always been about having fun together, we havent got any goals... really. We dont really care about our records, I mean we can get upset about a game or something, but we have lost sooo many games in our time that we cant really be bothered about it. I have a goal, and that is being seen as a fun clan to play against a clan that can take a defeat nicely and have good fun while playing even while losing and always shoot ppl that lag. Its kind of a clan motto.
 
6. I can certainly vouch for poff being fun to play :) Never a bad game with the poffers. How do you think this NQR has gone for you? You occupy a playoff spot at the moment, if you make it how do you think you'll do?
Well NQR is a really nice leauge, this NQR has been great for us, we ended with 16 played and 9 wins, which proves that div4 was just right for us, we have played some really nice games against some really nice clans. Only my undivided hate for Huli-Man could keep us from making an appearance in the next season. If we make it till playoff that will be great.
 
7. And if you don't?
always fun to play under competitve conditions, but if we dont, no biggie, we can always play AM matches against fips or some other clan.
 
8. What do you think about NQR as it is, e.g. the structure, schedule and map pool?
I think its really awesome, the funniest games are against clans in your skill range, and NQR aims at just that. scheduele have been good for us, and I think most clans manage well within it. The map pool question... hmm well I cant really be bothered to care as much as other ppl seem todo, throw some maps at me, and I will play them.
 
9. Who do you think will win the gold playoffs?
Well gazzie-boy, I think most ppl will think SR or LA will win this here little competition, but I really think that a little tribe called eq will rise to be the new star out there, lead forward by a strong man, jezaja!
na, just fucking with you, SR will win it.
 
10. You're a match admin for the upcoming QWWC. Are you looking forward to that event, and do you think your native Sweden can emerge victorious despite not using a single poff player?
I think the qwwc will be an intresting little happening, the ping will be a great factor tho... well sweden has a chance... but a lot could happen... and the choice by sassa not to use poff players will prove to be a wise one I think.
 
11. Ok some shorter questions now... TB3 or kenya?
Both
 
12. fips or hhh?
neither
 
13. :(
no wait, fips.
 
14. bacon or eggs?
eggs (vegetarian)
 
15. Britney or Christina?
Christina naked, in mud, with piglets sucking the sweet sweet milk from her.
 
16. Riiiiiight... gaz or Sassa?
Tough one, gaz got the sexy brittish accent, and Sassa got the zombie like swedish-english thing going for him. No w8 its easy: gaz.
 
17. That's the correct answer. Your money is in the mail... Thanks for taking the time to answer my questions sata, long my POFF continue to fuck furiously, strength and honour friend!
Thx for your time, Im amazed you took the time of beeing a qw world star to talk to me, a mere div4.
 
18. Well the way things are going, I'll be president of the world fairly soon, but I'll never forget the might of POFF. GG!
gg
