Battle Info  2004-10-31 3B - Week 8 (4)
 

 
Osams 2 - 1 Dybbuk
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



161-180

Reports
"SR spawns" is such a noob phrase; as Klatch will tell you, it's Dybbuk who are the experts at fullstarts on DM2 and we witnessed it here! To their credit they used their start well but we managed to fend them of at low to the point that Fox bored with 2nd or 3rd quad. We managed to take over with a few minutes to go and the scores were fairly close (30 frags behind with 3 mins to go) but they always had 1 or 2 RL guys so we couldn't quite clamp it down completely. GG! - by Osams

CMT4 - Andromeda 9



133-119

Reports
We played SSC earlier today (they had Milton) on this map and we played really well so we decided to go for it again here. We got more quads and the important 2 pents (2nd & 3rd) but they got control back with about 5 mins to go. They started to pull back our 40 frag lead but we went into uber-cs mode and hid for the rest of the game... sorry about that but all is fair in love and qw etc :) GG! - by Osams

CMT3 - DeutschMaschine



180-126

Reports
We threw E1M2 on the basis that they wouldn't know CMT3 that well and would probably prefer to play DM3 (our best map)... they shocked us by throwing DM3 and leaving us with the second kenya map. Luckily, myself and manny are well-versed in the ways of kenya and backed up well by our students lovevibration and lunnadoks, we were able to hold RA for most of the game and take the quads. We also all of the pents (I think) which kinda helped. GG, shame it had to end on CMT maps but it was not our intention :D *kram* - by Osams
