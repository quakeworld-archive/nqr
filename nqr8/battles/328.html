Veni Vidi Vici 2 - 1 Satanic Slaughter Clan
 
 

E2M2 - The Ogre Citadel



144-210

Reports
I demanded that we would play e2m2 against VVV since we sure didn't have our best lineup for the game. It seemed to be a good decision since VVV probably haven't played it much oppose to our few pracs. We manage to keep ra and quad most of the time. Seems like there was some bacon stuck in the servers CPU fan since the server and unexplainable pl peaks for everyone. We changed to iop after this. - by Satanic Slaughter Clan

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



150-147

Reports
VVV got pent and I fukked the jump from ring to quad. Seni got rl and managed get quad with first rl. Then we stayed for ra and went for the second quad. Can't remember what happened, but somehow VVV managed to lock down the map pretty good until after 12 minutes or so we did a come back. We were leading about 15 frags with 2 minutes to play. Then dragon managed to steal the quad from me equipped with 200/150. He killed my rl, then Slaughter rl later with that quadrun. Those were our only weapons and snake got one rl also so that was it. Pretty close :< - by Satanic Slaughter Clan
