Battle Info  2005-02-27 2 - Week 3 (3)
 

 
MPOS 2 - 1 American Idols
 
 

CTF8 - The Strongbox



1040-393

Reports
We got strength runes and stuff, held our base, snkfknrussian fucked their shit up with his mad hooking, yo. - by MPOS

This was MPOS's pick and we were confident at first but lost that as soon as we lost our short-lived lead (about 1 minute in). We put up a really good fight though. We just couldn't prevent them from leaving our base with our flag. We ended up with just 4 caps compared to their 17. MPOS was really able to take advantage of that weakness and that cost us big time. Also, I heard that WhitE did 'ok' controlling the quad and somehow beat everybody else, getting 64 kills. Though he did attack somebody point blank one time. :? indeed, WhitE =) - by American Idols


Demos
All Pov (MVD)

 
 

CTF2M3 - Spill the Blood



187-915

Reports
We didn't get any runes at the start, they got nearly all the quads and seriously fucked us up. - by MPOS

Our pick. Captures were 15-2 this time. Excellent quad control again. I think we held the runes most of the time. Basically a blowout again but for us this time. Unfortunately WhitE couldn't get 64 kills this time, so he settled for 63 with only 9 deaths. - by American Idols

E2M2 - The Ogre Citadel



452-447

Reports
We played with quad off, and we had control for most of the game. Then in the last few mins they started attacking with all their doods and got a few caps. With 15 seconds left they capped and we thought that was it. But no, they were still 5 frags behind so we all hid like shit and stuff. - by MPOS

This one wasn't a blowout. MPOS held the lead for a while but we started coming back at a steady rate near the end. We didn't attack with more than 2 people until the last minute. Download this demo, you will like it. Very nerve-racking. Since the demo upload feature seems to be broken, I put these demos up at: http://quakeworld.us/lemon/nqrctf1/ - by American Idols
