Battle Info  2004-02-17 Division 4 - Week 5 (3)
 

 
funky pirate superstars 2 - 0 Zetor
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



91-80

Reports
Fuck this was close! They got a good start, getting pent and RL, we got quad eyes and RA. Their first RL survived and so they had 2 for second quad, and they were able to hold RA pretty well. However they didn't really move out to YA and so we were able to get enough YAs and MEGAs along with lg/rl to attack quad and RA, and eventually we muscled our way back in (helped by a couple of pent steals). Then with 5 mins to go, they stole the last pent in cheeky fashion, and slowly regained the map. Vij fell off the RA platform with 4h and lost his RL, and we lost the RA, so it was time to hide in the water. It was as close as 8 frags I think but then dan and me got a couple more cheeky frags. Very good game! -gaz - by funky pirate superstars

CMT4 - Andromeda 9



108-101

Reports
Another hell of a close match! They got both armours/RLs at start, then pent, we got quad (but didn't use it well). Things were always very close ,with the two teams swapping over at the YA ledge. Many quads didn't make it out of the quad room (there was some awesome 4/5 man fights in there) , and at the end we managed to rush them all and took them out. We then got a hold of the ledge and used it to fend them off for the last few seconds, securing the win. These games are what QW is all about... pure adrenalin. Awesome stuff (especially when you win), it truly is the manly mans game. -gaz - by funky pirate superstars
