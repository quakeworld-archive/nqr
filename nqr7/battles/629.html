THERE'S NO PLACE LIKE 127.0.0.1 - by El?intarha 2.0

Our mapchoice started catastrophically and we spent the first 5-10 minutes runing around headlessly and getting owned by div0-ananas and friends. (You just KNOW things aren't proceeding as planned when all members at some point are standing together at RL). Still, the 2oo lead stayed at around 50 frags, to my surprise (we must have been doing something right since the lead didnt increase) and finally we managed to turn the game around thanks to a bit of teamplay and very nice quadruns by Offset.
