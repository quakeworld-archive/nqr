Battle Info  2004-11-28 3B - Week 7 (8)
 

 
Osams 2 - 0 Rabbit Dogs
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



217-124

Reports
Reports by gaz: They got fullstart and held the water/tele side of the map for the first 10 mins or so pretty well. I thought we were going to get raped, and the score was something like 80-15. Anyway, lovevib somehow managed to overtake tele between quads, he took next Q and cleared the map, and before too long we had 4 RLs and a full maplock, with lovevib sweeping RA/mega/low and myself running the quads, with manny/dirk doing nice work at the water/tele. We kept up this lock pretty well, denying them RLs and getting frags, and this was the key difference in the end. It could have gone either way I think and we did well to come back. GG! - by Osams


Demos
All Pov (MVD)

 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



146-137

Reports
We fullstarted (I got pent, manny RL, petter quad) and had the better start. RD are a really tough team to play against though, and they kept flooding RA and YA really well. We had control of the RA for the first 15 mins or so, when they got the last penta and took over, so we started to hide and slowspawn. We were a little fortunate at 3rd penta so combined with our good startspawns RD will be a little aggrieved to have lost here. GG! - by Osams

All Pov (MVD)
