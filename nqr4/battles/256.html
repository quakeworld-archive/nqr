Battle Info  2003-03-31 Division 1 - Week 3 (3)
 

 
The Axemen 2 - 0 Antiquad
 
 

E1M5 - The Gloom Keep



227-120

Reports
I was just back on irc, after the cb match, when diki asked for nqr. We felt lucky today, so 10minutes later, we were back in the game. With a small change in our lineup. tr0ll was to play his first nqr map. It started out well, we had control and everything seemed fine, except for the thing that aq didnt fall behind in scores. tr0ll was useless, as he had 125ping and 22 pl 90% of the game. And when Antiquad took over the map(!), we all felt kinda nervous. Could we bring this back, after loosing rl and sng, to strong aq's? tr0ll's lag didnt help much, but we took over sng, and placed him there, just to camp :p Aq was in the lead mid-game, but as we took over, we managed to take one of our better controls of the map. GL, RL and sng + quad. I dont think aq had many rl's in the end, and that made the score more uneven than it really was the first 12-15minutes. GG Antiquad, you were a real challenge, even with diki who said he'd never played the map before. :) - by The Axemen

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



191-100

Reports
Had a nice start here, even with AQ getting ring, quad and penta. Axe got rl, went to ya, and had some nice movements there, and continued to RA. We had now RA+rl early, as our rl guy was walking to sng for some health+ammo. SKVISJH, and he was telefragged :p 10secs later, AQ had rl on quad, and took over both RA and YA I think.. But we fought bravely, and took back RA, when mate suddenly dropped with no further notice. AQ said nothing, as we continued to play.. We kinda lost our consentration, as the game felt kinda easy. But despite of the fact that AQ only had 3 players, they had some nice moves, and caused us some problems. They finnished the map, and said gg. I must say, its a shame that this game kinda got ruined because of this. :/ Not only because we played 3on4, but also because this was AQ's map, and the fact that we had control when mate left, making the game very exciting. bg AQ :/ - by The Axemen
