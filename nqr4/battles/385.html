Battle Info  2003-03-24 Division 3 - Week 2 (2)
 

 
FUDOH 2 - 1 The Fighting Foes
 
 

E3M7 - The Haunted Halls



150-110

Reports
Well this game certainly had a strange start. After 15 minutes of play on e3m7, we noticed that mazer, with his -4 frags(!), was suffering from a weird and rare serverbug causing players to do only 1hp damage. Didn't matter if he had quad\rl\penta, he couldnt kill anything. Fortunately, Foes showed great sportsmanship and agreed to do a replay. After testing that Mazer could do some damage, we restarted the fight and got down to business. Foes got the better start, and had a 60 frag lead at the most i believe. However, solid teamplay and a few pentas turned the game. Must have felt nice for Mazerinen to finally get a few frags too =) GG by both teams. - by FUDOH

The first game we spawned the pent and secured end+rl and tock the tower with pent. The second game they spawned pent and tock over tower. We didnt ever get close to end-rl one time in the game. - by The Fighting Foes

Reports

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



164-156

Reports
As stated in our first report vs Cobots, we suspected Fudoh had some exciting and good games coming on dm2. This, just like the game vs QH, was to be one of those. Foes got the better start, and the game waved back and forth as it proceeded. With five minutes remaining, Foes had secured a staggering 40 frag lead. However ! Fudoh locked low rl, and shortly after tele\quad was safe too (why doesnt that happen more often). Foes, perhaps feeling somewhat overconfident with their lead, suddenly found themselves lost in the middle of heavy crossfire from Fudoh guns. We held our ground, playing calm and not stressing the frags, and eventually the game turned in our favour with a marginal 8 frags [even though stal did his standard quadbore in the final second =]. GG's Foes ! - by FUDOH
