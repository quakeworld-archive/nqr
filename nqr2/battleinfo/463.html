  2002-03-24  
Firing Squad vs America Must Die
     
  E1M2  
   
  317 - 63  
 
It was time for the playoff game between the Euro-clan Firing Squad and the Russian/Euro-clan America Must Die. E1M2 AMD's choice: Can't really remember who got first quad or rl, got distracted =(. at the 17th minute mark AMD got quad, but they could not put that much use in it, FS controlled Q and Gl/Ya, FS bagged a few AMD players and secured Mega aswell. At the 15th minute mark the score was 57-17 to FS. Raz became quadified and went on a hunt, he found his prey at ga/ssg area, he took them all down. He then went on to Ya to secure it, however he was greated by a Rocket in the face by AMD..FS quadrunner down. FS tooled them self up and went for q, AMD attacked Q from Ya FS quadrunner gets mowed down once again. Amd now controls Ya area, but FS retaliates through spikes and a furious battle over Ya is beeing faught. FS goes victorius out of the battle however. Amd steals a Q..but the glory doesn't last long..he gets gunned down almost instantly by FS. 12 min mark and the score is 100-23 to FS a clear indicator that FS got almost total control over the map. At the 9th min AMD manages to take Quad however the quadified AMD player isn't tooled enough, he send his fellow warriors to death by letting them jump first to Ya but they do not succed at the takeover attempt. Next FS Q bagges a few..he moves on to Mega but gets overpowered by the Amd welcome wagon..takes a few with him but the quadrunner gets gunned down eventually. Amd quad runner at 7th min has an argument with a wall..Quadrunner bore. Amd tries desperatly to gain some frags in the last minutes by gets turned down, FS now has full control over the map. 2nd minute mark score is 277-61, 1st minute mark score is 305-61. FF wins E1M2 with 317-63

  DM3  
   
  278 - 61  
 
FS Choice of map:DM3 FS get's a "fullstart" more or less, they get Quad, Pent and first RL. AMD is left with the Ring. 18 minute mark FS controles Ya,Ra and Quad, 15th minute mark score is 67-10 to FS. Amd launches an attack vs Pent area..But FS is well prepared and gets the 2nd Pent aswell. While FS is busy securing Pent Amd gets Quad, he cannot do much with it though. FS has almost a complete lockdown from time to time..and AMD tries to fight back all the time but they cannot hold on to any gained ground, cause of the quick retaliations from FS when they loose certain positions. The FS quadified player gets overwhelmed of the fact that he got quad and bores into a wall ;). Amd gets control over Ra with a RL tooled player, FS launches and attack with their quadrunner and is able to retake Ra area. Time for 3rd Pent, FS awaits the pent..and takes quad with 15-20 sec left to Pent. Amd has set up a trap at Pent water though...Quadified FS player gets electricuted at Pent window. A furious battle apears at Pent area almost involving all 8 players..it looks like Amd will go victorius out of the battle and get Pent when FS player steals the Pent 1mm infront of the AMD player (FS player had 5h left when he grabbed Pent), Amd spams "LOL LOL LOL LOL", and you really can't blame their anger. FS gets a lockdown on map..Amd gets some frags but nothing that will stop FS of winning this map also. 2 minute mark 243-56 to FS..1st minute mark 267-59 to FS. Game ends 278-61 to FS and FS advances to 2nd round in playoffs, GG both teams! Report by: Legio!
