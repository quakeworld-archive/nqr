Satanic Slaughter Clan 2 - 1 Oblivion
 
 

E1M2 - The Castle of the Damned



162-172

Reports
I can't understand what is it about SSC and e1m2. We had at least 3 rl's at a time but still we managed do lose them towards the end when Oblivion had at least one rl. They got the quads way2easily and that was it. - by Satanic Slaughter Clan

well, first of all the game was delayed for atleast 45 minutes cause of SSC having some serious hardcore trouble setting up new fuhquake (which in retrospect could have been done before the scheduled time). after that they slowly took control over the map and was in the lead with like 150-100, and im not sure how we managed to win. im still surprised as hell we actually did win it considering our form of the day :) - by Oblivion

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



243-34

Reports
We almost managed to get a fs with pent and quad/ring which was killed almost instantly by our superstars Slaughter and Stalk. I managed to get the second rl with pent and the second quad also. After that we managed to take most of quads and hold both ra and ya almost through the whole round. Oblivion was busy setting up cams for the decider map. - by Satanic Slaughter Clan

pepin had som lag issues at the start and was forced to drop which didnt exactly give us a dreamstart, and we made som serious fucked up n00b mistakes. SSC was relentless the first 10 minutes and at least i felt that they hade a little to good control over the map. Safe to say i wasnt in my best gaming mood and thus got pissed of at the 3-4 SSC specs, sry bout that :) ssc played really fkn well. // dje - by Oblivion


Demos
All Pov (MVD)

 
 

CMT3 - Deutschmaschine



242-99

Reports
Oblivion removed dm2 and we dropped e2m2. We got good start here also, enemy quad didn't manage to do much and we got the second rl. Can't remember what happened in the first 10 minutes of the game. I think we managed to get rest of the pents and hold ra and ya most of the time. Don't know much about what happened elsewhere, I just camped ya. I just remembered that this was the 300th official/league match for SSC. Hiphip! Hooray! Hiphip! Hooray! - by Satanic Slaughter Clan

we were "a little" pissed off about the dm3 game and basically played ffa for 20 minutes, not very bright considering ssc was at the top of their game today. gg again, puss och kram. - by Oblivion
