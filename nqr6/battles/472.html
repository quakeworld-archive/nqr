Battle Info  2004-04-04 Division 2 - Week 5 (8)
 

 
Bored With Life 0 - 2 Griffins Tappra Gossar
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



143-161

Reports
gg //oosi - by NQR Admin

Fullstart BWL! But boy, did we spam those RLs. Despite the first RL getting hurt real bad he managed to escape our grasp at first. Pent RL went down before him; chased into hill room, and despite getting the mega there he still died in 5 secs flat. Gangbang #1 complete! Gangbang #2 started around ring/quad/hill area and ended at 200-ng where he ended up giving our very own Ermac a pack with a delicious RL. Thank you, come again, bye now. We locked down RA shortly after that hectic start, not letting BWL even get close to it for the next 10-15 minutes. It wasn't until right before the 5 min pent when a quadrun went bad deep in enemy territory that BWL managed to turn the map around. They used their momentum to get that last pent and score an RA lock, which they shortly thereafter evolved to a full map lockdown. Four showscores later and 70 frags lead with 4 mins to go, we decide to play it safe. We played hide and seek for the rest of the game, which is why BWL got as close as they did. GG! /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



161-197

Reports
Ahh, not another fullstart BWL! RL, YA, Quad... oh, wait, their Quad died. Oh well, almost eh? Anyway, they got the start on E1M2, but weren't able to hold control for very long. Soon we had it down, keeping an rl or two at YA/GL area and running the quad towards ga/mega. But BWL were good, they kept fighting back, and with 12 mins to go the only 2 RLs on the map were in BWL hands. But skill can only buy you that much on a map like E1M2, and we kept YA/GL control on will and teamplay alone. Another minute later, there wasn't a single RL on the map! Score! We picked up the next few RLs and quads and managed to secure YA once again. With only 5 mins left or so, BWL had managed to get two RLs to mega and ambushed one of ours, gaining them three RLs to work with. But they were too slow; one pimped up quad incoming. One, two, three little RLs went down, and BWL lost their last chance to retake the map in time. When they managed to break through the YA defenses a few minutes later, we slid back to Mega and CS'd the rest of the game, securing our lead. GG! /dak - by Griffins Tappra Gossar
