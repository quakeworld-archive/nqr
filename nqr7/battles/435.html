Osams 2 - 1 PsychoHaloons Crew
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



213-53

Reports
This was their map, payed at splatterfest with minping 60. As you can see it's fairly even - budzior had laggy connection so he was spiking 80-90, but this was really as even as it could have been in the circumstances. We had zero TP, but manny showed us his Div1-calibre by holding tele, water and running quad all by himself. I went frag-hunting at low RL and got killed by enemies in big room a lot. GG ish. - by Osams

CMT3 - DeutschMaschine



79-211

Reports
They insisted we go to their "home" server for the second map... well np we thought. Until we got there. We all had over 65 ms, we were all spiking lots and had PL spikes. In short, it was unplayable for us, christ knows how they could claim that it was perfect... we decided to take CMT3 on the basis that they probably wouldn't know it and so we could offset the ping disadvantage that way. It didn't work cos they knew the map :D Big BG. - by Osams


Demos
-


  
 

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



178-73

Reports
We got an admin to come and fix last server because PHC refused to shift from their kurwa-server. Eventually we settled on Topsid where we had 10ms advantage and things were generally pretty fair. This map was won in the first 10 minutes when our superstars, lovevibraiton and gorbatjevtarzan managed to sew up GL and YA. Big BGs all round; amidst PHC trying to get 4 and then laming about the server situation, it took just under 3 hours from the scheduled start time until the end of the third map. :( - by Osams
