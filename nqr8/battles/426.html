DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



140-174

Reports
(gaz) I got low RL, they got quad/high, I retreated and CS'd at ra/mega. Don't remember what happened next, I think we got 2nd quad and we had a brief period of control of tele/water but after that it was sheer chaos. Every quad tightly contested and neither side ever controlling tele for more than a few minutes. They had much better aim in the fights and that played a big part in their win. These guys have great teamplay and with more practice they will be div 2 level 8) - by osams

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



133-123

Reports
Thriller game... - by NQR Admin

(gaz) I got RL, Kauka got pent, they got quad. Kauka died to quad just after he'd got 2nd RL. I got 2nd quad, don't remember what happened. For first 10 mins or so they were in control and I thought we were gonna lose 2-0. Somehow we pulled it round and tyrone started racking up the frags by camping YA/bridge while the other 3 of us rotated around RA. With a few mins left they somehow got back into it (despite us getting last pent/ring) and we had to slowspawn for the last minute to keep in the lead. - by osams
