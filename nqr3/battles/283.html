Battle Info  2002-09-22 Division 1 - Week 1 (1)
 

 
Clan Malfunction 2 - 1 Tribe of Tjernobyl
 
 

DM3 - The Abandoned Base



140-145

Report by CMF: Despite the start being quite even we quickly found ourselves behind by a few frags. Since we didn?t have a quadrunner playing, our chances of getting back into the game were slim. When the clock hit 15mins ToT was leading by around 50 frags and they even got the pent, so things were looking bad for us. Anyway, they probably were really excited as they were going to beat us on our homemap. They changed their playingstyle to a defensive one and that nearly cost them the victory. Our attempts to close the gap were almost successful, but no cigar, 145-140 ToT.

Demos
-


  
 

E3M2 - The Vaults of Zin



330-51

Report by CMF: Didn?t look too good going into this map. It was 2nd time for fix on the map and he didn?t quite know his way around the map, but enough of excuses! Dare got the first rl and secured ya relatively uncontested. Taking over rl-area wasn?t too hard as we had four rl?s after two minutes of play. We spawnfragged flawlessly for about 10mins and the game was over. ToT got a frag here and there but was never a threat, 330-51 cmf.

Demos
-


  
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



203-79

Report by CMF: ToT refused to agree on playing one of the known maps (e1m2,dm2) and wanted a random. I guess we both got what we wanted, ToT got their randommap and cmf got dm2 :) The first five minutes were quite even but then we got tele/quad-side of the map locked and made attacks to low-rl with quad. Our routine was enough to win here comfortably, 203-79 cmf. ggs tot!
