Battle Info  2003-03-23 Division 4 - Week 1 (2)
 

 
suicide commando 0 - 2 Unidentified Quake Entity
 
 

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



110-246

Reports
Let me start by congratulating sc for a nice game. First we played dm2, which was our choice. We started of pretty nice. After a little chaotic start, we quickly grabbed some sort of control. Especially low rl which was in the hands of UqE for almost the full 20 minutes. The tele was in their hands most of the times, but jesper could easily overtake tele several times because they didnt secure it properly. Alas the missing replacement failed us to keep tele in our control too. Quads were mostly UqE's, although not very much more then sc. I'd say about 12-8 in our favor. Most of those enemy quads however were quickly killed or at least wounded too much to do any realtime damage. All in all uqe had the most of the "control" on dm2, but sc stood their ground pretty well. :) GG to sc on dm2 :) - by Unidentified Quake Entity

E1M2 - Castle of the Damned



133-243

Reports
This was their choice of maps, obviousely. We lost first quad. :( After that there was no real controle by any of the 2 clans actually. Maybe a little sometimes at ya/gl but not too much and not very secure. So this map was won on individual better players and not on tp because neither used any in this map. A fairly exciting game with poor dragon getting a mere 2 rl's in the whole game. :( But still, good game bij SC. Overall sportive game and sportive clan to play against. We hope to play some more dewds! Good luck with the rest of ur games! :) - by Unidentified Quake Entity
