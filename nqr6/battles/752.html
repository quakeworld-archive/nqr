Battle Info  2004-02-08 Division 4 - Week 1 (1)
 

 
funky pirate superstars 2 - 0 OLw
 
 

CMT4 - Andromeda 9



188-59

Reports
Well OLW had never played this before so it was gonna be pretty hard for them to win it. Even worse, we got both RLs and quad/pent at the start so we had a decent grip on it pretty quickly. We sprinted into a lead. However we started to slow down in the second half and in the last 5 mins they took over the YA, stealing the pent and then getting a few quads to make the score a bit more respectable. -gaz - by funky pirate superstars

DM2 - Claustrophobopolis



160-127

Reports
oh dm2, foul temptress of the night, what a disloyal mistress you are to those who put their faith in you! after my sterling performance on cmt4 i dropped to allow the non-spiking (and more skillful) limey to play. olw got the best start, and i thought we would see a third map. they had a 30 frag lead and were controlling things nicely. i was speccing in autotrack so i dunno what happened, but all i know is that around ~8 mins or so, about 3 fips guys showed up at quad and stole it, and from there we clawed our way back. once we got ~30 frags ahead ourselves, the lead didn't change much and olw looked like they might take the map back, but it wasn't to be. -gaz - by funky pirate superstars
